| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | SMG |

**Pistol / 3rd-slot version:** [Uzi pistol](../pistols/uzi-pistol.md)

## Real-world notes

3.5 kg; 10.2 in barrel; telescoping bolt puts the mag in the grip, so the hand finds the mag by feel when reloading; grip safety; the mag sticks out below, which makes it awkward to fire prone; open bolt, so there's a slight delay between trigger pull and shot (Micro, Pro, and carbine are closed bolt); bayonet lug

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 9mm: 32 standard; real 40-round mag as makeshift extended and quick; real 50-round mag as extended quick; real 100-round Beta C-Mag drum as extended (a 70-round drum also exists). .45: 16 standard; real 22-round mag as makeshift extended and quick; 30-round Grease Gun mag (via a real conversion kit) as extended. Uzi Pro has native rails

## Versions

9 versions after merging. The 9mm guns split by size; every other caliber is one version covering all sizes. Skins: the early IDF and Dutch wooden stocks, and Germany's MP2 with its DES selector. The semi-auto Uzi pistol and Uzi Pro pistol moved to the pistol / 3rd slot.
