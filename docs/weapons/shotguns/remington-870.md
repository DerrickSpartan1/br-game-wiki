## Real-world notes

Best-selling shotgun in history as of 1983; over 11 million made; barrels from 14 to 30 in; screw-in Rem Choke system from 1986; shares parts with the Remington 1100 and 11-87; Norinco makes unlicensed copies

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 8 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Tube extension attachment; assault rifle family mags on the box-mag version; other attachments TBD

## Versions

8 versions after merging, out of hundreds of factory models. Rifled slug barrels merge in because slugs are an ammo type, and 3½ in Super Magnum chambers merge into the 12 gauge. Skins: Wingmaster walnut and blued steel, Express, camo turkey and waterfowl finishes, the corrosion-resistant Marine Magnum finish, and Norinco copies.
