## Real-world notes

3.27 kg; 18 in barrel in a short bullpup; sealed and waterproof; ejection ports on both sides (swap for lefties with partial disassembly; the cover on the unused port could leak gas into the shooter's face); reported to work flawlessly in Operation Cast Lead

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native rails. Unique: ITL MARS sight (red dot plus a visible or IR laser plus backup irons; IR laser fits night maps), Meprolight MP21 reflex sight, M203 40mm launcher, and the STAR-21 marksman kit (folding bipod plus ACOG 4×). Mag tiers: shares the M16's STANAG tiers (review later)

## Versions

7 versions after merging. The GTAR-21 (grenade launcher) and STAR-21 (bipod and ACOG) are attachment setups, so they merge into the TAR-21. Civilian 9mm and .300 Blackout models fold into those caliber versions. The Tavor 7 (7.62×51mm) went on the guns-to-add-later list for the battle rifle category.
