## Real-world notes

AK-pattern, gas-operated rotating bolt; 3.5 to 3.6 kg; two-position gas setting for standard and magnum shells; side rail for optics, later models add Picatinny rails; screw-in chokes and muzzle devices. PUBG's S12K is based on it

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Mag-fed shotgun, so it uses assault rifle family mags (drum as extended); other attachments TBD
