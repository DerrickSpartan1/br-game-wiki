## Real-world notes

Negev: 7.65 kg, 1,020 mm long with the stock out, 460 mm barrel, 915 m/s; NG-7: 7.6 kg, 1,100 mm, 508 mm barrel, 860 m/s; quick-change barrel, with the folding carry handle doubling as the barrel tool; the original skeleton stock lets it fire folded but not be cocked, while the newer stock folds the other way and works either way; the optic rail sits on the receiver, not the feed cover, so a scope keeps its zero through reloads; Vietnam's 7.62×39mm copy takes AK and RPK mags and RPD belts

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). One of the few LMGs with semi-auto, and the 5.56mm versions can fall back on AR mags. Every version has the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; Picatinny rail on the receiver. Unique: the assault grip (Commando versions), the bipod, and the 200-round belt box. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 150-round belt standard; 200-round box as extended; 35-round Galil mag as makeshift quick (5.56mm versions only); extended quick TBD
