## Real-world notes

4.25 kg; 1,090 mm long; 533 mm barrel; about 840 m/s; the gas piston sits above the barrel, and its tilting breechblock works much like the [SVT-40](svt-40.md)'s; adjustable gas regulator behind the front sight; a gas plug can shut the gas off for rifle grenades or manual operation; the recoil spring sits in the stock on fixed-stock rifles and in the receiver cover on folding-stock ones; the receiver hinges open on a pivot for cleaning; ramped aperture rear sight; light recoil on semi, but on auto it climbs off target fast; Commonwealth rifles (UK, Australia, Canada) were built to inch measurements, and the rest to metric

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 8 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)), so the L1A1 and civilian versions roll semi-auto only. Its auto fire climbs hard, which fits its recoil personality.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount, native rails on the modern IMBEL M964A1 and DSA SA58. Unique: rifle grenades through the gas plug, and the folding bipod built into the Austrian StG 58 and West German G1. Mag tiers (real mags, except extended quick): 20 standard; 30-round as makeshift extended and quick; 50-round drum as extended; extended quick TBD
