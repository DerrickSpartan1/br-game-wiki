# Armor and gear

Armor varies by more than tier, and each piece trades protection against speed or vision.

- **Helmets:** they come in levels, but the highest level partly blocks eyesight.
- **Vests:** a light vest is fast and keeps ADS (aim down sights) quick but leaves the arms unarmored; a vest with arm armor is heavier and slows ADS.
- **Legs:** no leg armor, since it mostly doesn't exist in real life.
- **Makeshift upgrades:** armor can be upgraded half a tier, like a tier 1 helmet to a makeshift tier 1.5 or a tier 2 vest to a makeshift tier 2.5. Makeshift tiers have lower durability but can take one extra bullet. Upgrades are crafted in-match from scrap.

Armor uses damage reduction like PUBG, with no plates. Players recover by healing.
