## Real-world notes

12.1 kg on its bipod; 1,219 mm long; 627 mm barrel; 765 m/s; trained crews swapped barrels in 10–15 seconds by swinging the receiver aside, wearing asbestos mitts; precisely machined, so it was slow and costly to make and sensitive to dirt, mud, and cold (flavor only); early guns had a rate regulator in the grip for 600–1,000 RPM; its tripod could "walk" fire up and down automatically

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The two-part trigger becomes semi and auto on the selector key. Every version but the civilian one has the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount. Unique: the 75-round saddle drum (it needs a feed adapter), the anti-aircraft sight, and the tripod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 50-round drum standard; 75-round saddle drum as extended; quick tiers TBD
