# AR-18

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 5.56×45mm (main) or 7.62×51mm |
| History | USA; designed at ArmaLite in 1963 by Arthur Miller's team as a cheaper alternative to the AR-15; made 1969–1985 by ArmaLite, Howa (Japan), and Sterling (UK); never a standard service rifle, but its gas system is the ancestor of most modern short-stroke rifles (G36, HK416, SCAR, AUG, SA80, and more) |

**Pistol / 3rd-slot version:** [AR-180SP](../pistols/ar-180sp.md)

## Real-world notes

3.0 kg; 18.25 in barrel; cheap stamped-steel build; side-folding stock that could be wobbly; the charging handle moves with the bolt and has a spring-loaded dust cover

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native dovetail scope mount. Unique: the original ArmaLite 2.75× scope on its quick-detach see-through mount. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; 25 makeshift extended and quick; the real 30-round Sterling mag as extended quick; the real 40-round Sterling mag as extended

## Versions

4 versions after merging. The semi-auto AR-180 far outnumbered the select-fire AR-18, so it should be the more common roll. The Sterling Sharpshooter (neoprene grip covers) and AR-180 SCS (wooden thumbhole stock, about 385 made) are skins. The AR-180SP pistol prototype moved to the 3rd slot. The AR-18 LSW prototype and the AR-18-based foreign rifles went on the guns-to-add-later list.
