## Real-world notes

An AR-15 layout with the [HK G36](g36.md)'s short-stroke gas piston in place of direct impingement, so gas stays out of the receiver; 3.12–3.85 kg on the A5; cold hammer-forged barrel rated for 20,000 rounds; free-floating rail on four sides that comes off without tools; 6-position telescoping stock; firing pin safety; an "over-the-beach" option lets water drain from the barrel; the A5 and later have ambidextrous controls and a tool-less adjustable gas regulator; in the Army's 2007 dust test it had 233 stoppages in 60,000 rounds against the M4's 882 (flavor only, since the game has no jamming); the M27 shoots about 2 MOA against the M16A4's 4.5 MOA; the real gun behind PUBG's M416, so most players already know it

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 6 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Like the [FN SCAR-L](scar-l.md), each barrel length is its own version. The M27's issue bipod and optics are normal attachments, not part of its version. It's its own family, with its own mastery track and cosmetics, separate from the [M16 / M4](m16-m4.md).

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native rails (top, sides, and bottom). Unique: the HK269 / HK269F 40mm underbarrel grenade launcher (standard on France's HK416F). Mag tiers: shares the [M16](m16-m4.md)'s STANAG tiers (review later)

## Versions

Norway's HK416N barrel lengths weren't on the sources checked, so its N and K are placed by name for now.
