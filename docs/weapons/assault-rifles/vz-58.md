## Real-world notes

2.91 kg, lighter than an AK; 15.4 in barrel; looks like an AK but shares no parts or mags with it; the bolt locks open on the last round; stocks swap by removing one bolt

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 8 versions, including a funny version (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; side dovetail mount on the Pi model, adapter mount otherwise. Unique: the NSP-2 infrared night sight (Pi model), which pairs with the night maps idea. Mag tiers: best guess, same pattern as the AK, review later

## Versions

8 versions after merging; the standard 7.62×39 vz. 58 should be the most common roll. .222 Remington and 7.62×51mm are new to the ammo pool. The P (fixed stock), V (folding stock), and Pi (night sight mount, bipod, cone flash hider) models are skins on version 1. The experimental bullpup, light machine gun, and marksman versions went on the guns-to-add-later list.
