## Real-world notes

2.4 kg; 17.75 in barrel; light and handy; criticized for weak stopping power and for jamming in extreme cold; 30-round mags could slant or fall out of early M1 mag catches; troops taped mags together "jungle style"

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount. Unique: the M3 infrared sniperscope (M2 only; real range 70 to 125 yd; needed a battery backpack) and the M8 rifle grenade launcher (22mm grenades). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 15 standard; 20 makeshift extended and quick; the real T3-A1 jungle clip (two 30s clamped together) as extended quick; the real 30-round mag as extended

## Versions

5 versions after merging. Since the semi-auto M1 made up most of the 6.1 million, it's the most common roll. 5.7mm Johnson is new to the ammo pool as an ultra-rare round. The M3's infrared sniperscope became an attachment for the M2. The pistol-sized Enforcer moved to the 3rd slot. The Ingram SAM rifles and the Hezi SM-1 bullpup went on the guns-to-add-later list.
