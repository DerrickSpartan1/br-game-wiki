## Real-world notes

M1918A2: 8.8 kg empty, 1,214 mm long, 610 mm barrel; the M1922 is about 11 kg; the bolt carrier acts as the striker, and the selector doubles as the safety; built for "walking fire" by advancing troops; the barrel is fixed on most models, so there's no quick barrel change until the FN Model D; no bayonet mount was ever issued; critics called the M1918A2's rate reducer a "Rube Goldberg device"; plastic stocks replaced walnut in 1942; production included 102,174 by Colt, Winchester, and Marlin-Rockwell in 1918–1919, 188,380 M1918A2s from 1941, and 61,000 during the Korean War

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It plays like a heavy automatic rifle rather than a belt-fed gun: 20-round mags, quick reloads, and the hard-hitting .30-06. The M1918A2's slow and fast auto rates sit on the selector key. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)). Every military version has the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount. Unique: the M1918A2's skid-foot bipod and flash hider, the monopod (dropped in 1942), the Colt Monitor's Cutts compensator, and the 40-round anti-aircraft mag. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; 40-round as extended; quick tiers TBD
