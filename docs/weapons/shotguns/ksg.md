## Real-world notes

Compact bullpup; loads and ejects downward through a port behind the pistol grip; Picatinny rails on top and under the forend; ambidextrous; each tube can hold a different load, like buckshot in one and slugs in the other

## In-game traits

Spawns with two rolled ammo types, one per tube, and switches between them with the select-fire button (the single-tube KS7 rolls one); one roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Tube extension attachment; rails for optics and accessories; other attachments TBD
