# Crafting

Crafting takes inspiration from Super People's system: finding stuff and building with it. It happens both during matches and back at your hub.

- **Scrap is the scavenger's money inside a match** (owner, 2026-11-24): "scrap to make makeshift stuff or heist stuff or pay for other things in the game for scavenger feel." It makes makeshift gear and heist tools at workbenches and pays for things during the match (radar stations, another comeback). [Cash](#cash) is what you take home.
- **Scrap is the crafting material** (owner, 2026-11-22; no longer the currency since 2026-11-24): there's one kind of scrap. You spend it at [workbenches](#workbenches) during a match, and whatever you haven't spent turns into cash when the match ends ([Cash](#cash)). Every scrap spent in a match is cash you don't take home, so spending is a real choice.
- **Out-of-game crafting:** scrap and quest items brought back to your hub, or to drop-off points, craft skins or turn in for quests. They go in their own pocket and don't add weight. Outside matches, scrap only buys looks: skins, quests, and other cosmetics, never anything that helps in a fight (owner, 2026-11-22).
- **Winning:** a win earns bonus scrap on top of whatever you brought out (our pick; the amount is TBD).
- **Taking things out of a match (owner, 2026-11-22):** everything you carry comes out with you, win or lose, except cash, valuables, and quest items, which go in the [cash bag](#cash) and have to be banked. A quest item counts only once it's stashed at a drop-off (owner, 2026-11-24), which replaces the earlier rule that it counted after you'd survived 5 minutes with it (owner, 2026-11-24: "get rid of the 5 minute timer for now, we will make drop offs decently available"). Skin-crafting items come out like scrap: in their own pocket, with no weight, kept win or lose (owner, 2026-11-22).

## Cash

Cash is the game's main currency, not scrap (owner, 2026-11-24): "instead of scrap being the main currency cash is, scrap held at end of match gets converted to cash, but cash can be found in vaults and safes and cash registers and other places for people to loot. this way you can give a heisty type feel to things."

- **Scrap converts:** any scrap you still hold when the match ends turns into cash. The rate is TBD.
- **Cash is loot:** found in vaults, safes, cash registers, and other places worth breaking into, so a match can be played as a heist.
- **What it buys:** looks only, the way scrap did: skins, quests, and other cosmetics, never anything that helps in a fight (owner, 2026-11-22, carried over). It can't be bought with real money or turned into real money (our pick), which keeps it clear of gambling rules.

The owner liked all of the first pass below (2026-11-24); details marked "our pick" are still to tune:

- **Where it's found (our pick, amounts to tune):** cash registers in shops, cafes, and gas stations (a little, opened at once with a ring of the drawer); safes in offices, the bank, the port's office, the prison, and better houses (more, cracked with your built-in tools in about 10 seconds, loud enough to hear about 40 m away); ATMs (broken open with a breaching tool, very loud); vaults (the big hauls); and bodies, which hold whatever cash their player carried. Amounts to tune.
- **Losing it (owner, 2026-11-24):** the heist needs a risk. Cash you carry drops with your body when you die, for anyone to take; you keep it by getting it out. Two ways out: win, or bank it at a cash drop-off on the way, where depositing takes a few seconds in the open. Drop-offs are decently easy to reach (owner, 2026-11-24); our pick for the first map is about one in each big town, town, and spot, about 20 in all, like the workbenches. Banked cash is yours even if you die later. A [cash balloon](match-flow.md#mini-heists) made from scrap banks the bag from anywhere, if nobody shoots it down (owner OK, 2026-11-25).
- **The cash bag (owner, 2026-11-24):** "maybe they have like a little gym bag they carry on their back under their backpack and you dont have to worry about carry weight." Cash goes in a small gym bag worn on your back under the backpack, with its own space, so it never counts against carry weight or crowds out gear. It holds as much as you want (owner, 2026-11-24): "its that much more of a risk if you dont stash it." The more you carry without banking it, the more you lose if you go down. It looks fuller as it fills, so other players can see who's worth chasing (our pick), and it drops with your body like the cash in it.
- **Quest items (owner, 2026-11-24):** "thats where quest items go and quest items have to be stashed." Quest items ride in the cash bag too, and only count once they're stashed at a drop-off; carried unstashed, they drop with your body like the cash.
- **Mini heists:** themed heists on the map, from Payday, are the big sources of cash and valuables ([Mini heists](match-flow.md#mini-heists)).
- **Valuables (owner, 2026-11-24):** jewelry and other valuables are loot too. They all get converted to cash (owner: "it all gets converted to cash"). Our pick: they go in the cash bag with the cash, and each is worth a set amount of cash when it's banked or when the match ends: rings, necklaces, watches, gold chains, diamonds, paintings, and similar, found in houses, shops, safes, and vaults. [Gold bars](match-flow.md#gold-bars) are the big payout and the one valuable with weight: each slows you down (owner, 2026-11-25). [Paintings](match-flow.md#paintings) are cut from their frames (owner's idea, 2026-11-25).
- **In the match (owner, 2026-11-24):** wanted contracts pay cash; radar stations and workbenches still take scrap.

## In-match crafting

In-match crafting happens at workbenches: tables around the map where you spend scrap to make things (owner, 2026-11-22). This is what the scrap machine idea became, and there's no crafting from the backpack. It's the scavenger's fallback: rough versions of things you haven't found yet, always worse than the real thing.

### Workbenches

First pass (our pick, 2026-11-22), scaled from Apex Legends' replicators and Warzone's buy stations:

- **How many:** about 20 on the first map: 2 or 3 in each big town, 1 or 2 in each town, 1 in most villages, and 1 each at Object 7, the Kolkhoz, and the quarry. Apex's maps start with 12 replicators and get 2 more later.
- **Using one:** stand at the bench while it works; walking away cancels the craft and gives the scrap back. One craft at a time per player, with no limit on how many.
- **Noise:** power tools and hammering, heard about 30 m away, so players nearby know someone's crafting. Apex alerts nearby players the same way.
- **Breaking things down:** a bench also turns things you don't want into scrap, like Rust's recycler: 10 for a gun, 5 for an attachment, and 5 to 15 for armor depending on its tier. It takes 3 seconds.

### Earning scrap

- **Scrap piles** in the loot pool ([Loot](loot.md)): 5 scrap each, or 10 in workshops and garages. Apex's supply bins give 5 crafting materials each.
- **Breaking things down** at a bench (above).
- **Bodies** keep the scrap their player carried.
- **Vaults** hold 50 scrap, and care packages 25.
- **What a match is worth:** a player who loots steadily should finish with roughly 100 to 200 scrap if they spend none (a target to test).
- **Carrying it:** scrap goes in its own pocket with no weight (changed from taking backpack space when it was the currency).

The cheapest item to the most expensive is 10 to 150 scrap, close to Warzone's buy stations ($1,500 to $10,000). [Radar stations](match-flow.md#radar-stations) charge 50 scrap.

Boarding up doors and windows with planks is gone: doors are [nailed shut](gameplay.md#nailing-doors-shut) with the built-in hammer, and windows stay open.
