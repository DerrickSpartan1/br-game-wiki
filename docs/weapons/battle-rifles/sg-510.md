| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |

| History | Switzerland; after the gas-operated SK 46 and the blow-forward AK 53 fell short, SIG's Rudolf Amsler designed the AM 55, borrowing the roller-delayed system of Mauser's wartime Gerät 06H and StG 45(M); adopted in 1957 as the Stgw 57 and issued to every Swiss soldier as his personal weapon, replacing the K31 rifle, the Army's submachine guns, and the Lmg 25 light machine gun; more than 700,000 made 1957 to the mid-1980s; replaced by the [SIG SG 550](../assault-rifles/sg-550.md) from 1990; the 7.62×51mm SG 510-4 was exported to Bolivia and Chile, where it saw use in the 1973 coup; Monaco's palace guard also carried it; Swiss soldiers could keep their rifle after service, and about 40% did as of 2007 |

## Real-world notes

Heavy at 5.6–5.7 kg, which soaks up recoil; 1,100 mm long; 583–609 mm thick barrel (sources differ); about 750 m/s; mostly stamped sheet metal; straight-line stock with a recoil buffer inside; stepped, fluted chamber; the integral 26 mm muzzle brake cuts recoil by about 25% and doubles as the rifle grenade launcher; perforated barrel jacket with two folding bipod positions (near the muzzle or near the receiver), and the bipod legs are marked as an inclinometer for aiming grenades; large T-shaped charging handle; S/E/M selector (safe, single, automatic); a fold-down winter trigger works with arctic mittens; solid rubber butt and a big carry handle at the balance point; tall folding iron sights adjustable from 100 to 640 m; the Swiss Army rated it at 6 cm mean dispersion at 300 m from a rest

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 6 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Its weight and muzzle brake make it one of the steadier full-power rifles on auto, which fits its recoil personality. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)), so the civilian versions roll semi-auto only.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native quick-detach scope mount. Unique: the Kern 4×24 scope (tritium reticle, bullet-drop knob for 100–800 m), Chile's Supra 4×24 on the SG 510-4, the two-position bipod, Gewehrgranaten 58 rifle grenades (anti-armor, fragmentation, and smoke; up to 400 m with a booster), a night-vision sight with an IR light (fits night maps), and the bayonet. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 24 standard; 30-round Lmg 25 mag as makeshift extended and quick; higher tiers TBD (the SG 510-4 starts at 20)
