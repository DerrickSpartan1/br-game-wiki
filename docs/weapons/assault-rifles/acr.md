## Real-world notes

3.6–4.4 kg; 660 mm long with the stock folded, 900 mm fully extended; in real life the barrel swaps without tools, and the caliber changes by swapping the bolt head, barrel, and mag; hammer-forged, nitrided barrel, free-floated in the handguard; non-reciprocating charging handle that fits on either side; ambidextrous safety, mag release, and bolt release; the Remington's selector has safe, semi, and auto; right-side ejection with a case deflector for left-handed shooters; stock options: folding six-position, fixed adjustable, or a Magpul PRS-based sniper stock; full-length top rail; the Remington has a five-sided aluminum railed handguard, the Bushmaster a polymer handguard with heat guards and an aluminum tri-rail; borrows from the [AR-18](ar-18.md), [FN SCAR-L](scar-l.md), [HK G36](g36.md), XM8, and [M16](m16-m4.md); named after the Siege of Masada; launched at $2,685–$3,061, about twice the early quotes, which turned many buyers away; in October 2010 Bushmaster recalled every ACR for a slamfire flaw that could fire several rounds on one pull (flavor only); best known from Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 (2009), where it appeared under a deal with Remington that the Wall Street Journal reported in 2023 (no money changed hands)

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 10 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Like the [FN SCAR-L](scar-l.md), its real barrel and caliber swaps don't happen mid-match: each barrel length and caliber is its own version, so the spawned version is the gun you keep. The off-caliber versions were only made as semi-auto Bushmaster civilians, so they roll semi-auto only.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native top rail, plus handguard rails (five-sided on the Remington, a tri-rail on the Bushmaster). Unique: none found. Mag tiers: 5.56 versions share the M16's STANAG tiers; other calibers TBD (review later)

## Versions

10 versions after merging. No production numbers were found for any of them; the 16.5 in 5.56 rifles were the main models. Caliber conversion kits (Remington's 6.8 SPC kit, Templar Precision's 7.62×39 kit) aren't separate versions, since the game has no conversion kits; they're listed under the factory rifle of the same caliber. A 6.5mm Grendel version was planned but never made.
