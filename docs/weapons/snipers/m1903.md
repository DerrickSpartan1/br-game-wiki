## Real-world notes

3.94 kg, 1,097 mm long, 610 mm barrel. The .30-06 fires a 150-grain pointed bullet at about 880 m/s (the original .30-03 fired a 220-grain round-nose at about 670 m/s). The M1903A3 of 1942 uses stamped parts and a peep sight on the receiver. The M1903A4 carries an M73B1 2.5× scope. The Pedersen device replaces the bolt and turns the rifle into a semi-auto firing a pistol-size round from a 40-round mag that sticks out at about 45 degrees, with slots to see how many rounds are left. 65,000 devices, 101,775 Mark I rifles, 1.6 million mags, and 65 million rounds were made from 1918 to 1920. The Army destroyed nearly all the devices after 1931, and fewer than 100 survive.

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Shares .30-06 with the [M1 Garand](../battle-rifles/m1-garand.md) and the [BAR](../lmgs/bar.md). Reloads with a stripper clip from empty, or one round at a time to top off. The Pedersen version is the odd one out: a semi-auto with a 40-round mag.

## Attachments

DMR & sniper family attachments; adapter scope mount (the M1903A4 comes scoped). Unique: the M73B1 scope and the Pedersen device. Mag tiers: none on the stripper-clip versions (ammo loops speed up top-offs); the Pedersen device's 40-round mag is its only mag
