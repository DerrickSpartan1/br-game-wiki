## Real-world notes

2.1 kg; built on the AKS-74U and shares 60% of its parts with the AK-74, including the trigger, selector, and side-folding stock; the helical mag doubles as the lower handguard and is often mistaken for a grenade launcher; 64 rounds because Makarov ammo comes in boxes of 16; some mags have holes to check the round count; the helical mag had reliability issues

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; AK side mount for optics (adapter mount). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 9×18: 64 standard, the real early 67-round aluminum mag as makeshift extended, other tiers TBD; 9×19: 53 standard; 7.62: 35 standard; other tiers TBD

## Versions

5 versions after merging. Skins: the Bizon-3's over-the-top folding stock and peep sight, and the Vietnamese SN9P's Galil-style stock. The 7.62 version can also load 7.63mm Mauser under the shared ammo rule. Its box-mag successors, the PP-19-01 Vityaz and PPK-20, went on the guns-to-add-later list.
