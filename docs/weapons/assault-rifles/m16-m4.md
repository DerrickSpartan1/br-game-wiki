| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle |

## Real-world notes

M16A1 2.89 kg, M16A2 3.40 kg, M4 2.92 kg; 20 in rifle barrel, 14.5 in on the M4, 10 to 11.5 in on Commandos; light recoil and very accurate; the straight-line stock cuts muzzle rise

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions, including a funny version (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native rail on the M16A4, M4, and M4A1, adapter mount on the carry handle of the M16A1 and A2. Unique: M203 or M320 40mm underbarrel launcher, Masterkey or M26 underbarrel 12-gauge shotgun, 22mm rifle grenades fired straight off the flash hider with no adapter, and the M234 non-lethal riot launcher (tear-gas ring rounds). Mag tiers (all real mags): 30 standard; 40-round as makeshift extended and quick; 60-round as extended quick; 100-round Beta C-Mag as extended

## Versions

9 versions after merging, split mainly by length and fire mode. .300 Blackout is new to the ammo pool. The SEALs' Mk 4 Mod 0 (waterproofed, suppressed) and the Norinco CQ's revolver-style grip are skins. The 9mm Colt SMG, the marksman versions, and the AR-15-based foreign rifles went on the guns-to-add-later list.
