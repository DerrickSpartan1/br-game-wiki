## Real-world notes

1.13 kg; 5.5 in barrel (3.9 in on the Bolo); 425 m/s in 7.63mm; wooden shoulder stock doubles as a holster. Full list in [Versions](#versions) below

## In-game traits

One roster entry; when it's picked, the match also rolls one version. The Schnellfeuer stays in the pistol slot

## Attachments

No scope. All versions get barrel, magazine, and shoulder stock slots; the stock slot takes the real wooden stock if it spawns, or a makeshift one

## Versions

21 versions after merging, down from 48. Each row is a design and cartridge combination that actually existed; versions with the same round and only small differences are merged, with the differences noted. Rarity follows the combined Made numbers.

Versions that differ only in markings or grips become skins: the Ottoman, Italian Navy "slabside", Persian, and Luftwaffe contract guns, the French Gendarmerie model, the unmodified Brazilian PASAM, and the Chinese arsenal copies. The merged makers (Astra, Royal/ETAI, Azul, Federal Ordnance, and modern replicas) can become skins too.
