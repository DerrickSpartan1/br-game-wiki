## Real-world notes

M10: 2.84 kg, 5.7 in barrel; M11: 1.59 kg; telescoping bolt with the mag in the grip; threaded barrel; strap under the muzzle for recoil control; poor accuracy, famously called fit only for combat in a phone booth; the .380 can empty 32 rounds in about two seconds

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments. Unique: its suppressor doubles as a foregrip, making full auto easier to control, and .45 is naturally subsonic, so a suppressed .45 is extra quiet. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): .45 uses Grease Gun mags and tiers; 9mm: 32 standard, 40 makeshift extended and quick, 50 extended quick, extended TBD; .380: 16 standard, 24 makeshift extended and quick, the real 32-round mag as extended quick, extended TBD

## Versions

4 versions after merging; the MAC-11 joins the MAC-10 as one family, so both count toward the same weapon mastery. Semi-auto pistols moved to the 3rd slot as the MAC pistol. Related but separate designs for later: South Africa's BXP, Rhodesia's Cobra carbine, and Brazil's Enarm MSM. To research: the semi-auto Mk III, IV, and V in .30, .41, and .50 Ingram, unusual calibers sold in South America.
