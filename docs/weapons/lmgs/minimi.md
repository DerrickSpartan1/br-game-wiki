## Real-world notes

Standard: 7.1 kg, 1,040 mm long, 465 mm barrel, 925 m/s; M249: 7.47 kg, 1,035 mm, 915 m/s; Para: 7.44 kg, 914 mm long (766 mm with the stock collapsed), 349 mm barrel; Minimi 7.62: 8.2 kg, 502 mm barrel; a trained gunner swaps barrels in 6–7 seconds; belt and mag feed switch instantly, with the belt covering the mag well when in use; the bolt carrier acts as the striker; its feed mechanism comes from the MAG, which drew on the MG 42

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The classic squad gun: light, quick, and steady. The 5.56mm versions with a mag well can fall back on AR mags (see [Attachments](#attachments)). Auto only, with the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; Picatinny rail on the feed cover of later versions (C9A1 and up), railed handguards on the Mk 46. Unique: the three-height bipod, the C9A1's ELCAN C79 3.4× scope, and the M192 tripod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 100-round belt standard; 200-round belt or box as extended; 30-round STANAG mag as makeshift quick (5.56mm versions with a mag well only)
