## Real-world notes

3.9 in barrel; 820 g; 305 m/s; weak locking design, and the left side plate loosens after prolonged firing; only safety is a small lever in front of the grip; fixed blade and notch sights. Skin: the Brixia Model 1912, adopted by the Italian Navy as the Mod. 1913 (strengthened frame, no grip safety; 5,000 made)

## In-game traits

More damage per shot than the M17, to make up for its small magazine and otherwise worse time to kill

## Attachments

No real-world accessories. In-game: pistol/SMG barrel attachments (probably), pistol/SMG mags (maybe); suppressor and sight TBD after seeing how it fires, since either may make it too powerful given its higher damage
