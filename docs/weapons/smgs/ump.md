## Real-world notes

2.3 to 2.5 kg; 7.9 in barrel; mostly polymer; closed bolt makes it accurate, but its simple blowback action makes it harder to control than the MP5; slow fire rate keeps .45 recoil manageable; side-folding stock; bolt locks open after the last shot; in 2000, HK recalled some because the polymer charging handle could break off

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; four native Picatinny rails (top, both sides, bottom). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): .45: 25 standard, 30 makeshift extended and quick, higher tiers TBD; 9mm and .40: 30 standard, 35 makeshift extended and quick, higher tiers TBD

## Versions

4 versions after merging. Triggers are fixed per version (game choice, not historical): the UMP45 and UMP9 use the standard semi/auto trigger; the UMP40 gets the burst pack (semi and 2-round burst); the USC is semi-only. Semi-auto UMP-clone pistols moved to the 3rd slot as the UMP pistol. Skin: the USC's original grey finish.
