## Real-world notes

M1891/30: about 4 kg, 1,232 mm long, 730 mm barrel, about 865 m/s. The M1891 infantry rifle has an 800 mm barrel. The carbines are 1,013 mm long with 514 mm barrels: the M38 weighs 3.4 kg and fires at about 710 m/s, and the M44 weighs 4.1 kg, fires at about 725 m/s, and has a side-folding spike bayonet. Sniper rifles were hand-picked for accuracy, with scope mounts on the left and bent-down bolt handles, using PU 3.5× and PEM scopes. The US-made rifles of 1916–1917 have walnut stocks, and many stayed in the US for training and civilian sale. Finland's rifles used its own 7.62×53mmR loading.

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 8 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). PUBG added a Mosin later; here it shares 7.62×54mmR with the [SVT-40](../battle-rifles/svt-40.md), [SVD](../battle-rifles/svd.md), and [PK](../lmgs/pk.md). Reloads with a stripper clip from empty, or one round at a time to top off.

## Attachments

DMR & sniper family attachments; adapter scope mount (the sniper version comes scoped). Unique: the PU and PEM scopes and the M44's folding bayonet. Mag tiers: none, since it's fed by stripper clips; ammo loops speed up top-offs.
