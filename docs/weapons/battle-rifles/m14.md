# M14

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Battle rifle |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 7.62×51mm (main) or 7.62×39mm |
| History | USA; developed at Springfield Armory from the [M1 Garand](m1-garand.md) as the T44, with refinements by Lloyd Corbett, a shorter action for the new T65 round (which became 7.62×51mm NATO), and a 20-round detachable box magazine; it beat Earle Harvey's T47 (ex-T25) and the [FN FAL](fn-fal.md), tested as the T48, and was adopted in 1957; 1,376,031 made 1959–1964 by Springfield Armory, Winchester, Harrington & Richardson, and TRW; the M16 replaced it as the standard rifle in 1967, since it was long and heavy for Vietnam's jungle, its wood stocks swelled in the humidity, and its auto fire was hard to control; it stayed in training and in Europe until 1970, served as the M21 sniper rifle until 1988, and came back in Iraq and Afghanistan as a marksman rifle and the Mk 14 EBR; still carried by the Army's Old Guard and other ceremonial units; Taiwan built over a million under license as the Type 57 from 1969; donated in large numbers to Estonia (40,500 in 1998) and Ukraine (35,000 in 2022); the Mk 14 EBR is PUBG's crate-only Mk14 |

## Real-world notes

4.2 kg empty, 4.9 kg loaded; 1,126 mm long; 559 mm barrel; about 853 m/s; a gas cut-off system replaced the Garand's long operating rod; aperture rear sight; walnut stocks, then birch, then fiberglass; its full-power round made auto fire climb hard, so most rifles were kept on semi; the Mk 14 EBR puts the action in a Sage aluminum chassis with four Picatinny rails and a collapsible stock, but critics in 2017 called it nearly 15 lb loaded and front-heavy; navy ships still use an M14 to fire a rubber line-throwing projectile between ships

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 8 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Its auto fire climbs hard, which fits its recoil personality. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)): the M14, M14A1, and Mk 14 Mod 0 are select-fire, and the marksman, sniper, and civilian versions are semi-auto only.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount, native rails on the Mk 14 EBR and other chassis versions; the sniper version spawns with its scope. Unique: the M76 rifle grenade launcher with the M15 grenade sight, the M2 bipod, the M6 bayonet, and the Mk 14 EBR's Harris bipod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; higher tiers TBD
