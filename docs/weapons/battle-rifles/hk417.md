## Real-world notes

4.4 kg (16.5 in A2) to 4.74 kg (20 in A2); 994–1,082 mm long; cold hammer-forged, chrome-lined barrels with a 1:11 twist, changeable in under two minutes with simple tools; optional accurized barrels reach 1 MOA with match ammo; H&K says the piston keeps gas out of the receiver, so the bolt group runs cleaner and cooler than on direct-impingement ARs; an early prototype used G3 mags, which have no bolt hold-open; the A2 refined the receiver, barrel interface, gas port, and bore alignment; the G28 is guaranteed to group within 45 mm at 100 m (1.5 MOA), has a steel upper, and shares about 75% of its parts with the HK417, at about $12,000 a rifle; the M110A1 swaps in an aluminum upper to save weight, at 3.81 kg unloaded

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 6 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Each barrel length is its own version. The G28 and M110A1 spawn with a scope, since each was issued as a scoped rifle. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)): the Assaulter, Recce, and Sniper are select-fire, and the G28, M110A1, and civilian versions roll semi-auto only.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native Picatinny rails (STANAG 4694 rails on the G28, M-LOK handguard on the M110A1). Unique: the G28's Schmidt & Bender 3–20×50 PM II and 1–8×24 PM II scopes, the M110A1's Schmidt & Bender 3–20×50 PM II Ultra Short and SIG TANGO6 1–6×24 scopes, its OSS SRM6 suppressor, and the 6–9 in Harris bipod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard (10-round also real); higher tiers TBD
