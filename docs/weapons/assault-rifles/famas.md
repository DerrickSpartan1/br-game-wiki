# FAMAS

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle (bullpup) |
| Cartridge | 5.56×45mm |
| History | France; developed 1967–1971 at MAS under General Paul Tellié; French service rifle from 1978, being replaced by the HK416F since 2017; about 400,000 F1s and 10,000–15,000 G2s made; nicknamed "Le Clairon" (the Bugle) for its shape |
| Magazine (rounds) | 25-round proprietary box (F1) or 30-round STANAG (G2) |
| Fire mode / rate | Semi, 3-round burst, and full auto (built in: a second selector behind the magazine picks burst or full auto); about 900–1,100 RPM, one of the fastest-firing rifles in the roster; lever-delayed blowback |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Real-world 300 m (F1) or 450 m (G2); in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

3.61 kg (F1), 3.8 kg (G2); 19.2 in barrel; bullpup with a carry handle over the top; built-in folding bipod; tritium night inserts in the rear sight (fits night maps); reversible cheek rest for lefties; the F1 was built around French steel-cased ammo and disposable mags, and reused mags or brass ammo caused malfunctions; some plastic parts were fragile

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter mount on the carry handle, rails on later versions. Unique: rifle grenades up to 500 g with two built-in grenade sights for direct fire (75–100 m) or angled indirect fire (rifle tilted 45° for 120–340 m or 75° for 60–170 m), M203 underbarrel launcher, and a bayonet. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): F1 starts at 25, G2 at 30 (the G2 could share the M16's STANAG mag tiers)

## Versions

4 versions after merging. All keep the real semi, 3-round burst, and auto modes. The FA-MAS Type 62 (an earlier 7.62×51mm prototype) and the Croatian HS Produkt VHS bullpup went on the guns-to-add-later list.
