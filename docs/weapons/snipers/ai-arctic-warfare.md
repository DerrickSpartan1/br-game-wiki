## Real-world notes

Base AW: 6.5 kg, 1,180 mm long, 660 mm barrel, 850 m/s, and built to work down to −40 °C. AWM: 6.9 kg, 1,270 mm long, with a 686 mm (.338) or 660 mm (.300) barrel and 936 m/s (Germany's G22 is rated at 885 m/s). The AWS is built for subsonic ammo, with a 406 mm barrel and a suppressor (711 mm together) and about 300 m of range. The AWC Covert folds into a small case with a 305 mm barrel and suppressor. 35 suppressed PM-SDs went to UK special forces.

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). PUBG's AWM is the .300 Magnum version, which stays on the ground here. The .338 Lapua version is crate-only, since its head shot kills through a tier 4 helmet ([Starting damage](../../systems/starting-damage.md)). Every version reloads by swapping mags. The AWS and AWC keep their suppressors ([Built-in parts](../../systems/attachments.md#built-in-parts)).

## Attachments

DMR & sniper family attachments; native scope rail. Unique: the AWS and AWC suppressors and the folding stocks. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 10 standard (7.62×51mm) or 5 (magnums); others TBD
