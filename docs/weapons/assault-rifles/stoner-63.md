# Stoner 63

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle (its belt-fed configurations go in LMGs later) |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 5.56×45mm (main) or 7.62×51mm |
| History | USA; designed in 1963 by Eugene Stoner, L. James Sullivan, and Robert Fremont; made by Cadillac Gage 1963–1971, about 4,000 total; one receiver plus swappable parts turns it into a rifle, carbine, automatic rifle, or machine gun; used by Navy SEALs in Vietnam and tested by the Marines |
| Magazine (rounds) | 30-round box (20-round also offered); the Automatic Rifle feeds from the top |
| Fire mode / rate | Select-fire, about 700 to 900 RPM; rifle and carbine fire from a closed bolt (no delay); the Automatic Rifle fires from an open bolt and is full-auto only, so it gets the open-bolt delay |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Sights to 500 m on the rifle; in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

Rifle 3.58 kg, Automatic Rifle 4.62 kg; heavier than similar rifles because of its modular design; 20 in barrel (15.7 in on the carbine); quick-detach barrels; complex and maintenance-heavy, which is why the Army rejected it

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount (the Dutch 63A1 had a quick-release scope mount). It also had a 40mm grenade launcher accessory and a folding bipod. Mag tiers: best guess, review later

## Versions

5 versions after merging. Only the magazine-fed configurations are here. The belt-fed LMG, tripod-mounted MMG, vehicle-mounted fixed machine gun, and SEALs' Commando (Mk 23 Mod 0) go in the LMG category later. The Stoner LMG from Knight's Armament and the Robinson M96 went on the guns-to-add-later list.
