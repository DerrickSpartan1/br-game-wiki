# Snipers

One category: bolt-action, lever-action, and heavy precision rifles, in rough order of when each came out. Semi-auto marksman rifles go to [battle rifles & DMRs](../battle-rifles/index.md). Lever guns count here, like PUBG's Win94.

Most snipers here reload one round at a time or by stripper clip, and pressing fire stops a one-round-at-a-time reload ([Ammo](../../systems/ammo.md#how-ammo-and-reloading-work)).
