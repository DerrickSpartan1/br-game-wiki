## Real-world notes

2.6 kg; 10.4 in barrel; bullpup, so very short for its barrel; fully ambidextrous; ejects casings downward; built-in reflex sight with backup irons; 5.7mm has about 30% less recoil than 9mm, a flat trajectory, and defeated body armor in real use

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 3 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; takes a suppressor. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 50 standard and quick; no real larger mag, so extended tiers TBD. The P90 has a built-in reflex sight; the TR has rails for any scope

## Versions

3 versions after merging, all in 5.7×28mm, which is new to the ammo pool. Skins: the USG's aluminum sight housing and reticle, olive drab frames, and the clip-on pouch that catches spent casings. The FN Five-seven pistol uses the same round and went on the guns-to-add-later list.
