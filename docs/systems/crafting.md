# Crafting

Crafting takes inspiration from Super People's system: finding stuff and building with it. It happens both during matches and back at your hub.

- **Out-of-game crafting:** materials and quest items found in a match can be brought back to your hub, or to drop-off points, to craft skins or turn in for quests. These go in their own pocket and don't add weight.
- **Winning:** a win earns a bonus crafting item on top of whatever you brought out.
- **Dying:** you lose half the skin-crafting items on you. Most quest items stay with you once you've survived a certain amount of time from the start of the match, so the system can't be cheesed.

## In-match crafting

In-match crafting happens at workbenches: tables around the map where you spend scrap to make things (owner, 2026-11-22). This is what the scrap machine idea became, and there's no crafting from the backpack. It's the scavenger's fallback: rough versions of things you haven't found yet, always worse than the real thing.

### Workbenches

- **Where (our pick):** in workshops, garages, farm sheds, and at the Military Outpost, a few in each town.
- **Using one:** crafting takes time, like healing, and you stand at the bench while it works, so you're exposed.
- **Scrap** is loot ([Loot](loot.md)) and takes backpack space. It also pays for [radar stations](match-flow.md#radar-stations).

### What a workbench makes

- **Improvised attachments:** a makeshift suppressor that's louder than a real one and wears out after a set number of shots, or taped mags for faster reloads. Helps when a rolled gun has few matching attachments.
- **Basic meds:** bandages from cloth, or a splint from scrap. Full med kits stay loot-only.
- **Armor repair:** patch a damaged vest or helmet to restore some durability.
- **Makeshift armor upgrades:** half a tier up, like a tier 2 vest to a makeshift tier 2.5 ([Armor](armor.md)).
- **Molotovs and noisemakers:** bottle, fuel, and cloth make a molotov; scrap makes a decoy that draws players toward a sound.
- **Vault drill (later):** an expensive way to open a [vault](match-flow.md#vaults-and-keys) without a key.

Boarding up doors and windows with planks is gone: doors are [nailed shut](gameplay.md#nailing-doors-shut) with the built-in hammer, and windows stay open.

Scrap costs, craft times, and how many workbenches a map has are TBD. Whether there are different kinds of scrap is still open.
