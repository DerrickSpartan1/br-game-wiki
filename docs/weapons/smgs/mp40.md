## Real-world notes

3.97 kg; 9.9 in barrel; first SMG with a forward-folding metal stock; resting bar under the barrel for firing over vehicle sides; the single-feed mag could jam, especially when used as a handhold; about 1.1 million made; the Allies called it the "Schmeisser," though Schmeisser didn't design it
