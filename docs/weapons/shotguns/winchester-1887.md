## Real-world notes

Rolling-block lever action; 3.6 kg; one of the first successful repeating shotguns. Winchester insisted on a lever-action for brand recognition, though Browning suggested a pump. Built for black powder shells; the Model 1901 was strengthened for smokeless powder. 64,855 Model 1887s and 13,500 Model 1901s made; modern versions by Norinco and Chiappa

## In-game traits

A normal shotgun, not a funny gun, balanced like the others; one roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)
