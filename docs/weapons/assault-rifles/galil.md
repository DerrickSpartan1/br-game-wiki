## Real-world notes

SAR 3.75 kg, AR 3.95 kg, ARM 4.35 kg; heavier than an AK because of its milled steel receiver; AK reliability with better accuracy; flings empty cases up to 40 ft; built-in tritium night sights (pairs with night maps); the ARM's bipod is a wire cutter (possibly able to cut wire fences in-game) and its handguard has a bottle opener so troops stopped opening bottles with their mags

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 10 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount on the classic Galil, native rails on the ACE. Unique: rifle grenades off the slotted flash suppressor (it had a dedicated 12-round blank mag for launching them), M7 bayonet, and the ARM's bipod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 35 standard; 40 makeshift extended and quick; the real 50-round mag as extended quick; the real 65-round mag as extended

## Versions

10 versions after merging, covering both the classic Galil and the modern Galil ACE. 5.45×39mm is new to the ammo pool through the limited ACE run. The Galatz / Galil Sniper, SR-99, and Marksman Assault Rifle went on the guns-to-add-later list. Decided: the ACE shares the Galil family (same action and mags, like the AK-103 in the AK family), so it shares Galil mastery and cosmetics.
