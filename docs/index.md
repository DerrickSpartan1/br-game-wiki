# Battle Royale Game Wiki

A PC battle royale that brings back the feel of original 2018 PUBG, with one twist: the guns and ammo change every match.

- **Platform:** PC only, not mobile.
- **Feel:** scavenging, a slower pace, and deep guns and attachments, like PUBG in 2018.
- **Signature hook:** no two matches have the same gun and ammo pool.
- **Build approach:** built solo with AI-assisted coding until it's functional; real developers come later.
- **Release:** early access at $30.

**Guiding rule:** grounded, but not all the way. The game should stay fun and easy. No single gun should win every fight.

## How this wiki works

Claude maintains this wiki from conversations with the game's designer: new guns, decisions, and ideas get filed on the right page, and every change is logged in the [changelog](planning/changelog.md). Real-world specs come from the sources listed on each page; anything marked TBD hasn't been decided yet.
