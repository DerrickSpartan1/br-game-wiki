# Crafting

Crafting takes inspiration from Super People's system: finding stuff and building with it. It happens both during matches and back at your hub.

- **Out-of-game crafting:** materials and quest items found in a match can be brought back to your hub, or to drop-off points, to craft skins or turn in for quests. These go in their own pocket and don't add weight.
- **Winning:** a win earns a bonus crafting item on top of whatever you brought out.
- **Dying:** you lose half the skin-crafting items on you. Most quest items stay with you once you've survived a certain amount of time from the start of the match, so the system can't be cheesed.

## In-match crafting

In-match crafting is the scavenger's fallback: rough versions of things you haven't found yet, always worse than the real thing.

- **Improvised attachments:** a makeshift suppressor that's louder than a real one and wears out after a set number of shots, or taped mags for faster reloads. Helps when a rolled gun has few matching attachments.
- **Basic meds:** bandages from cloth or a splint from scrap. Full med kits stay loot-only.
- **Armor repair:** patch a damaged vest or helmet with scrap to restore some durability.
- **Molotovs and noisemakers:** bottle, fuel, and cloth make a molotov; scrap makes a decoy that draws players toward a sound.
- **Barricading:** board up a door or window with planks.

Crafting materials take up backpack space, and crafting takes time, like healing.

Makeshift armor upgrades are also crafted in-match from scrap. The different kinds of scrap are still to be worked out.
