## Real-world notes

3.63 kg; 18.9 in barrel; mostly carbon-fiber-reinforced polymer; side-folding stock; folding ambidextrous charging handle; brass deflector for lefties; famous overheating problem: a 2015 report found its hit rate fell to 7% at 100 m once the gun heated up by 30 °C+, which led Germany to retire it (flavor only: no overheating in-game)

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions, including a funny version (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; optics sit in or on the carry handle (rails on later versions). Unique: the ZF 3×4° dual optic carry handle (3× scope with an unmagnified red dot on top, bullet-drop marks to 800 m, auto night illumination), the export 1.5× carry handle scope, Hensoldt NSA 80 night sight that clamps to the carry handle (fits night maps), AG36 40mm launcher, rifle grenades, and bayonets (often leftover East German AKM bayonets). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 30 standard; real 100-round C-Mag as extended; the clip-together studs could be the quick tier
