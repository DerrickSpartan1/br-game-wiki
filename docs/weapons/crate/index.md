# Crate weapons

An exclusive pool of guns too powerful or special for the ground pool. They arrive by air drop.

Every crate holds one crate weapon, its ammo, attachments that fit it, one tier 3 vest, one tier 3 helmet, and one class item ([Loot and spawning](../../systems/loot.md#changes-from-pubg)). Heavy and anti-materiel sniper rifles, like .50-caliber ones, go here, not in [snipers](../snipers/index.md).

What goes here: anti-vehicle and heavy sniper rifles (a head shot kills through any helmet, like PUBG's AWM), lethal grenade launchers and lethal launcher rounds, and belt-fed guns too fast for the ground ([Weapon pool](../../systems/weapon-pool.md#how-the-randomness-works)). The flash shield and rocket launchers are crate-only too, as [class items](../../systems/class-items.md).

Crate-only versions of ground guns, marked `(crate)` on their pages: the .338 Lapua Magnum [M24A3](../snipers/remington-700.md) and [AWM .338](../snipers/ai-arctic-warfare.md), the .300 Winchester Magnum [M2010 and Remington 700](../snipers/remington-700.md) and [AWM .300](../snipers/ai-arctic-warfare.md), and the [MG 34/41](../lmgs/mg-34.md) for its fire rate ([Starting damage](../../systems/starting-damage.md)).

The whole [MG 42 / MG3](mg-42.md) family is crate-only: its 900–1,500 RPM belt-fed versions kill faster than ground LMGs are allowed to, even with their damage cut, and PUBG keeps its own MG3 in care packages ([Starting damage](../../systems/starting-damage.md#crate-check-for-fast-firing-guns)).

Queued: the Barrett M82 / M107 (.50 BMG), the Accuracy International AW50 (.50 BMG), and the CheyTac M200 (.408 CheyTac). The RPG-7 is queued as a crate-only class item.
