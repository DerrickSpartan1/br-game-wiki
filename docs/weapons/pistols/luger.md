## Real-world notes

Toggle-lock action; 871 g; 3.9 in barrel (P08), 4.7 in (Model 1900), 5.9 in (P04 Navy), 7.9 in (LP08); about 3 million made. Main versions: Model 1900, Model 1902, P04 Navy, Model 1906, P08, LP08 Artillery (long barrel, board stock, snail drum), carbines, Swiss 06/29, Bulgarian and Finnish contracts, .45 trial guns (two known)

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Snail drum as the extended mag (from the LP08); other attachments TBD

## Versions

7 versions after merging. 1920s commercial guns came with barrel lengths from 75 to 600 mm on request; those fold into the nearest version. Skins: wartime finishes such as the Bakelite "Black Widow" grips, French- and East German-assembled guns, Portugal's Model 943, and the Bolivian contract guns.
