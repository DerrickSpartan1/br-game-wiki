## Real-world notes

3.6 kg with the 20 in barrel (3.2–3.3 kg on carbines); bullpup, so a full-length barrel in a short gun; locks open on empty; switching the bolt and ejection-port cover makes it left-handed; "silent cocking" forward assist; trigger often called mushy

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 11 versions, including a funny version (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; the AUG A1's built-in Swarovski 1.5× scope (ring reticle, backup irons, low-light dots) can't be swapped, every other version has rails. Unique: M203, AG36, AG-C, or SL40 40mm underbarrel launchers, 22mm rifle grenades off the flash hider (20 in and 16.4 in barrels, using the gas valve's grenade setting), and an M7 or KCB-77 bayonet on 20 in barrels. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 30 standard; 35 makeshift extended and quick; 38 extended quick; real 42-round mag as extended

## Versions

11 versions after merging, including a funny version. Triggers (game choice): most versions use the progressive trigger, played as semi/auto on the normal fire-mode selector key; the AUG Carbine gets semi plus 3-round burst; the civilian version and F88T trainer are semi-only. The AUG HBAR (heavy barrel, bipod, open bolt) goes in the LMG category later, and the experimental AICW went on the guns-to-add-later list.
