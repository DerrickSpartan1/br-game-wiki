## Real-world notes

Mk II: 3.2 kg, 7.7 in barrel; extremely cheap (about $11 vs. $200 for a Thompson); can fire if dropped while cocked; fires without lubrication; the single-feed mag can misfeed, especially when held by the mag; the metal barrel sleeve heats up fast

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 32 standard; 40 makeshift extended and quick; 45 extended quick; the real 50-round Lanchester mag as extended. The suppressed version can't take barrel attachments

## Versions

5 versions after merging. Skins: the Mk I's wooden foregrip and flash hider, the Canadian skeleton stock, and the German MP 3008's bottom-mounted mag. Prototypes left out for now, which could become rare versions later: Mk IV, T42, Rofsten, Viper, Small Arms Ltd. Model 2 (50-round belt-feed mag), the corner-shooting swivel-stock Mk V, and Taiwan's Type 90 with a folding sword blade. Related guns that could be added on their own later: Austen and Błyskawica.
