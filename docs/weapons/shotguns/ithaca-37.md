## Real-world notes

Loads and ejects through the bottom, popular with left-handed shooters; longest production run of any pump-action shotgun; over 2 million made by 2003; SEALs used a duckbill shot diverter for a flatter, wider spread; some military guns had a ventilated handguard and bayonet adapter

## In-game traits
