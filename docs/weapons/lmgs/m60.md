# M60

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | LMG (general-purpose machine gun) |
| Cartridge | 7.62×51mm (all versions) |
| History | USA; developed from 1952 to 1957 as a lighter 7.62mm replacement for the M1919, borrowing the general-purpose idea of the [MG 34](mg-34.md) and [MG 42](mg-42.md), with an operating system from the [FG 42](../battle-rifles/fg-42.md) (early tests used a modified captured FG 42); standardized in 1957 and issued from about 1960; it served with every branch of the US military, most famously in Vietnam, where troops called it "the Pig" for its bulk and appetite for ammo, and later in Laos, Cambodia, The Troubles, Lebanon, El Salvador, Grenada, Panama, the Gulf War, Afghanistan, and Iraq; replaced or supplemented in most US roles by the [M240](fn-mag.md); still made and upgraded, and Denmark ordered 600+ M60E6s after it beat the HK121 in 2014 trials; other users include South Korea, Taiwan, Indonesia, and the Philippines |
| Magazine (rounds) | 100- or 250-round M13 disintegrating belts |

## Real-world notes

10.5 kg; 1,105 mm long; 560 mm barrel; 853 m/s; Vietnam-era complaints included jams when dirty, torn case rims, a barrel latch that could catch, and runaway fire from sear wear (flavor only); the original's bipod sits on the barrel, so a barrel change takes the bipod with it until later versions moved it; the gas piston could be put in backwards during cleaning; in 1994–95 Army trials, the M240 went 2,962 rounds between stoppages to the M60's 846

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). A slow, steady full-power belt gun. The military versions are auto only, with the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount (rails on the Mk 43 Mod 1). Unique: the short assault barrel and the Mk 43's composite handguard. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 100-round belt standard; 250-round belt as extended; quick tiers TBD
