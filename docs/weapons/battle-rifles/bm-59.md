## Real-world notes

4.4 kg; 1,095 mm long; 491 mm barrel; its Tri-Compensator muzzle device is a muzzle brake, flash hider, and rifle grenade launcher in one; folding bipod on the gas cylinder; a folding grenade sight doubles as the gas cut-off for grenade blanks, and firing a grenade with the gas still on damages the rifle; folding winter trigger; folding shoulder plate like the [M14](m14.md)'s; the charging handle rides on the operating rod and moves with each shot; cleaning kit stored in the stock

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It's its own family, separate from the M1 Garand: a different maker's gun on the same layout (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)). Versions keep their real fire modes, so the civilian version rolls semi-auto only.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount. Unique: the Tri-Compensator rifle grenade launcher with its folding grenade sight, the folding bipod, and the bayonet. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; higher tiers TBD

## Versions
