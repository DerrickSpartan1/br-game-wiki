## Every gun page has

- **Spec table:** category, cartridge, history, magazine, fire mode and rate, damage, and effective range. Effective range in-game means full damage before falloff.
- **Real-world notes:** the details that shape how the gun should feel.
- **In-game traits:** how it plays and how many versions it rolls.
- **Attachments:** its attachment family, scope mount, unique attachments, and mag tiers.
- **Versions:** the merged versions table, with what was merged and how many were made.
- **Sources:** the pages the specs came from.

Shared rules for all guns are on the [Rules](../rules.md) page, attachment families and mag tiers on [Attachments](../systems/attachments.md), and calibers on [Ammo](../systems/ammo.md).
