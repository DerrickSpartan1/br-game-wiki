## Real-world notes

AK-47: 3.47 kg; AKM: 3.1 kg; 16.3 in barrel; famously reliable in dirt and harsh conditions; 7.62×39 punches through foliage, walls, and car bodies (fits the building-material system); milled AK-47s are a bit more accurate than stamped AKMs; steel mags tough enough to use as hammers

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; side-rail scope mount on night-sight (N) models and the AK-103, adapter mount otherwise. Unique: the GP-25 40mm underbarrel grenade launcher (AKM and later) and a cup-type launcher that fires regular RGD-5 hand grenades out to about 150 m. Mag tiers (best guess, all real mags): 30 standard; 40-round RPK mag as makeshift extended and quick; 50-round box as extended quick; 75-round drum as extended

## Versions

7 versions after merging; the AKM should be the most common roll, and the balanced-recoil AK-109 a rare one. 7.62×39mm and 5.56×45mm are new to the ammo pool. Skins: the Romanian PM md. 63's built-in vertical foregrip, laminate and polymer furniture, and the Yugoslav M70. The RPK goes in the LMG category later. The Galil, Beryl, and vz. 58 already have their own spots in the AR queue, and the INSAS is a possible addition.
