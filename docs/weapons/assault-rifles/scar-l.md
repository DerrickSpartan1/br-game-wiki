# FN SCAR-L

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 5.56×45mm (main) or .300 Blackout |
| History | Belgium / USA; designed by FN Herstal in 2004 for USSOCOM's SOF Combat Assault Rifle program and built by FN Manufacturing in Columbia, South Carolina; reached SOCOM units in April 2009 as the Mk 16, beside the 7.62mm SCAR-H (Mk 17); in June 2010 SOCOM cancelled further Mk 16 buys after 850, saying it wasn't different enough from other 5.56 rifles, and pulled them by 2013 while keeping the Mk 17; Navy special operations fielded it the most; still used by Belgium, Portugal, Chile, Peru, Ukraine, and 20+ users in all; FN ended civilian SCAR production in October 2025, except the SCAR 15P, then brought out a next-generation civilian line in 2026 |
| Magazine (rounds) | 30-round STANAG box (same as the M16); 10-round on some civilian models |
| Fire mode / rate | Semi and auto, about 625–650 RPM; fully ambidextrous controls; short-stroke piston with a rotating bolt, closed bolt, so no open-bolt delay |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Real-world 500 m (400 m on the CQC, 200 m on the SCAR-SC); in-game TBD |

**Pistol / 3rd-slot version:** [FN SCAR 15P](../pistols/scar-15p.md)

## Real-world notes

3.29 kg; 14 in barrel on the standard model; one-piece aluminum upper with a full-length top rail, plus side and bottom rails; polymer lower; side-folding stock adjustable for cheek height and length of pull; charging handle mounts on either side; flip-down front sight; in real life its three barrel lengths (10, 14, and 18 in) swap in the field; built to share parts and ergonomics with the [SCAR-H](../battle-rifles/scar-h.md), so both feel the same in the hand; one of the rifles PUBG players know

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 6 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Its real barrel swaps don't happen mid-match: each barrel length is its own version, so the spawned version is the gun you keep.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native rails (top, sides, and bottom). Unique: the FN40GL / Mk 13 EGLM 40mm launcher on the bottom rail (swing-out breech, double-action trigger). Mag tiers: shares the M16's STANAG tiers (review later)

## Versions

6 versions after merging. The real field-swap barrels become separate versions. The 7.62mm [FN SCAR-H](../battle-rifles/scar-h.md), with the Mk 20 SSR and SCAR 20S marksman rifles, has its own page, and the HAMR automatic rifles went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list. The Mk 17's 5.56 conversion kit is a version on the SCAR-H page, since it's still a Mk 17.
