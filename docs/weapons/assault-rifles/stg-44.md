# StG 44

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 7.92×33mm Kurz (main) or .22 LR (modern replica) |
| History | Germany; designed 1938 to 1943 by Hugo Schmeisser; made 1943 to 1945; 425,977 made; the first successful mass-produced assault rifle and a big influence on the AK-47; still turned up in the Syrian civil war |
| Magazine (rounds) | 30-round curved box (troops loaded 25 to spare the spring) |
| Fire mode / rate | Select-fire, about 500 to 600 RPM; gas-operated with a closed bolt, so no open-bolt delay (except the MKb 42(H) version) |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Real-world 300 m on auto, 600 m on semi; in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

4.6 kg; 16.5 in barrel; inline design keeps full auto controllable; robust in cold, dirt, and snow; troops were told to fire mostly semi-auto and short bursts

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 3 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; game-version scope mount. Unique attachments: the ZG 1229 Vampir infrared night scope (15-minute battery) and a rifle grenade launcher (looting TBD). Possible: the Krummlauf curved barrel with a periscope sight for shooting around corners (wears out after about 300 rounds). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 30 standard; 35 makeshift extended and quick; higher tiers TBD

## Versions

3 versions after merging. MP 43, MP 44, and StG 44 were different names for essentially the same rifle. 7.92×33mm Kurz is new to the ammo pool. The StG 45(M), Volkssturmgewehr 1-5, and MKb 42(W) went on the guns-to-add-later list.
