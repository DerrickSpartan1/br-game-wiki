## Real-world notes

4.3 kg with scope and empty mag; 1,225 mm long; 620 mm thin, chrome-lined barrel; about 830 m/s; looks like an AK on purpose, to keep the same handling, but it's a different design with a machined receiver; two-position gas regulator that can be turned with a cartridge rim; skeleton thumbhole stock with a detachable cheek rest, in birch plywood until black polymer took over in the 1980s; a permanent slotted flash hider and a bayonet lug, so this sniper rifle takes an AKM or AK-74 bayonet; iron sights to 1,200 m stay usable with the scope on; the 7N1 sniper round (1966) is held to about 1–1.2 MOA of vertical spread, and Soviet estimates gave about a 50% hit chance on a standing man at 800 m and 80% at 500 m; the 7N14 round (1999) added a hardened steel core for body armor; a stuck floating firing pin could fire a round on its own in poor maintenance or extreme cold (flavor only)

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 11 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The military versions spawn with their scope; the iron sights still work alongside it. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)), so only the SVU-A and the V-70 roll with auto.

## Attachments

DMR/sniper family attachments; native Warsaw Pact side rail (Picatinny on the SVDM). Unique: the PSO-1 4× scope with bullet-drop chevrons, the NSPU and NSPUM night sights (fit night maps), the AKM or AK-74 bayonet, the SVDM's 1P88-4 variable scope, bipod, and quick-detach suppressor, and Poland's LD-6 6×42 scope. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 10 standard; the V-70's 15- and 20-round mags as makeshift extended and extended; extended quick TBD

## Versions

11 versions after merging. Foreign copies that differ only in small details merge with the SVD. The experimental SVDG (a 10 mm smoothbore firing a 3 mm dart), the 6mm SSV-6, and the AF (an automatic 5.45mm prototype that took AK-74 mags) are left out for now. Romania's PSL, Yugoslavia's M76, and Serbia's M91 look similar but are different designs: the PSL is already a possible addition on the [queue](../../planning/queue.md), and the rest went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list with the Chukavin SVCh.
