## Real-world notes

MG 42: 11.6 kg, 1,220 mm long, 530 mm barrel, 740 m/s; MG3: 11.5 kg, 1,225 mm, 565 mm barrel, 820 m/s; stamped parts made it far quicker to build than the MG 34 (75 man-hours); barrels swap in under 10 seconds, and regulations called for a change after about 150 rounds of rapid fire (flavor only, since the game has no overheating); its fire was so fast that single shots were hard to hear

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The fastest-firing gun in the category: it shreds up close but empties its belt quickly. Auto only, except the MG 74. Every version has the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount. Unique: the anti-aircraft sight, the tripod, and the MG3's 100-round drum. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 50 standard; 100-round drum as extended; quick tiers TBD
