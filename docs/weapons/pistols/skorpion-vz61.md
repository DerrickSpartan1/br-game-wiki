## Real-world notes

1.30 kg; 4.5 in barrel; a rate reducer in the grip slows it from 1,000 to 850 RPM; wire stock folds up over the gun like a scorpion's tail and it fires with the stock folded or extended; carried in a holster like a pistol; ejects straight up; bolt locks open after the last shot

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below); in the 3rd slot because it was carried in a pistol holster

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; takes a suppressor. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): .32: 20 standard (a 10-round short mag also exists); 25 makeshift extended and quick; real 30-round aftermarket mag as extended quick; extended TBD. .380 and 9×18: 24 standard; real 30-round mag as makeshift extended and quick; higher tiers TBD

## Versions

4 versions after merging, one per cartridge. The whole Škorpion sits in the pistol / 3rd slot, since it was carried in a pistol holster. Semi-auto civilian guns (the CZ-91S and Czech Small Arms' modern vz. 61 pistol) merge into their caliber's version, with the CZ-91S's stainless receiver as a skin. 9×18mm Makarov is new to the ammo pool, and the Stechkin APS and PP-19 Bizon later in the queue use it too.
