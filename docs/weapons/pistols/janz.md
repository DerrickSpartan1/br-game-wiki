## Real-world notes

Premium, up to €25,000 each, about 100 made a year; started by a former Korth parts supplier; quick-change models swap caliber cylinders and barrels; custom engraving and gold inlays. Full list in [Versions](#versions) below

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls a version. 4 versions; see [Versions](#versions) below. No mid-match cylinder swaps: the caliber it spawns with is the caliber it keeps

## Attachments

Revolver reload attachments (speedloaders and similar); caliber cylinders and barrels on EM and E frames; higher-capacity cylinder as extended if one exists

## Versions

4 versions after merging. The quick-change models share the EM and E frames, so every EM and E frame Janz can swap caliber cylinders and barrels in real life; the MA frame has no quick-change version. In-game, each spawns in one caliber and keeps it. The fixed-barrel frames are documented as caliber ranges, not exact lists.

| # | Version | What's different | Cartridge | Merged from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | EM frame | Small frame; 2.5 to 12 in barrels; can swap caliber cylinders and barrels | .22 LR up to .44 Magnum | EM, EM-S quick change |
| 2 | E frame | Medium frame; 2.5 to 12 in barrels; can swap caliber cylinders and barrels (.22 LR, .32, .38, 9mm Para, .357 Magnum, .44 Magnum, up to .454 Casull). Merged difference: the sport models have adjustable sights | .22 LR up to .454 Casull | E, E-S quick change, sport revolvers (.357 and .44 Magnum) |
| 3 | MA frame | Large frame; 4 to 12 in barrels | .460 S&W | — |
| 4 | MA frame | Same | .500 S&W | — |

Quick-change barrel lengths: 3, 4, 5, 6.5, 7, 8, 8.75, 10, and 12 in. Janz also makes a 9mm semi-auto, the Janz-Schuknecht Pistole, which could be its own pistol entry later.
