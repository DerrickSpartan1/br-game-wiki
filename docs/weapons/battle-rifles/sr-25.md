## Real-world notes

M110: 6.27 kg, 1,029–1,181 mm long depending on stock and suppressor, 508 mm barrel, about 783 m/s with M118LR match ammo; the original SR-25 Match has a 610 mm heavy, free-floating barrel, since the handguard attaches only to the receiver; about 60% of its parts are shared with the AR-15 and M16; two-stage match trigger; built around 168-grain match ammo, with accuracy guaranteed at 1 MOA or better (0.5 MOA on the Enhanced Match); the M110 adds a modular rail with a folding 600 m backup sight, ambidextrous controls, and a quick-detach suppressor; in 2011 the Army asked to rework the M110 after complaints about field performance and constant parts replacement (flavor only); the M110 ranked second on the Army's top ten inventions of 2007

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The Mk 11 and M110 spawn with a scope.

## Attachments

DMR/sniper family attachments; native Picatinny rail (URX rail on the M110). Unique: the M110's quick-detach suppressor, the Mk 11's Harris bipod and Leupold Mark 4 mil-dot scope, the one-piece 30 mm KAC scope mount, and the folding 600 m backup iron sight. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard (10-round also real); higher tiers TBD
