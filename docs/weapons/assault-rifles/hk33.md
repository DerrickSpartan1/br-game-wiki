## Real-world notes

HK33A2 3.65 kg, HK53 3.05 kg; 15.4 in barrel (8.3 in on the HK53); fluted chamber to help extraction; heavier than an M16; modular push-pin stocks, handguards, and trigger packs; the stock HK33 doesn't lock open on empty

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; HK claw scope mount (same as the MP5). Unique: HK79 40mm underbarrel launcher, rifle grenades off the slotted flash hider (full-length rifle only), detachable bipod, and the G3 bayonet. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 25 standard; real 30-round mag as makeshift extended and quick; 35 extended quick; real 40-round mag as extended

## Versions

7 versions after merging. The 4× scope ZF models are skins on version 1. The HK33SG/1 marksman rifle and the HK13, HK23, and Myanmar MA-12 machine guns went on the guns-to-add-later list. Triggers (game choice, not historical): HK33, T223, Type 11 bullpup, and HK32 use the standard safe/semi/auto trigger; the HK33K gets the 4-position trigger (semi, 3-round burst, auto); the HK53 gets the burst pack (semi and 2-round burst); the civilian version is semi-only.
