# Sound list

Every sound the game needs, for the sound worker to work from (owner asked, and OK'd the list and its ranges, 2026-11-24). How sounds are found, licensed, and layered is on [Gun art and sound](art-and-sound.md#sound); the rules for how sound behaves in a match are on [Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md#sound). Each line needs several takes, so nothing repeats.

**Heard by others** is how far away another player can hear it, before walls. "You" means only the player making it hears it. Footstep ranges are PUBG's from before its 2019 footstep change, the 2017–2018 feel ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/enemies-can-hear-your-steps-in-pubg-from-this-distance,307161)); gunshot ranges follow PUBG's own design (sniper shots up to about 1 km, shotguns 700 m, handguns 400 m; [PUBG, Getting a Sense of PUBG Pt. 4](https://devtrackers.gg/pubg/p/b2a9efd9-getting-a-sense-of-pubg-pt-4)). Every other range started as our pick, and the owner OK'd them all (2026-11-24).

## How far sounds carry

- **Walls and floors:** a sound heard through a wall or floor carries half as far and sounds muffled (our pick).
- **Surfaces:** loud surfaces (metal, wood floors, gravel, roof tiles) carry footsteps a quarter farther; soft ones (grass, sand, carpet) a quarter less (our pick).
- **Rain** carries footsteps a quarter less, and thunder halves how far gunshots carry for about 3 s ([Weather](../systems/match-flow.md#weather)).
- **Direction:** every sound others hear comes from where it was made, so it can be placed by ear.

## Weather and the island

All of these are ambience: everyone hears their own, from where they stand.

- **Wind:** by height and openness (stronger on the plateau and the coast).
- **Rain:** light and heavy, on the ground, on roofs (louder indoors), on tin, on water, and on vehicles.
- **Thunder:** near cracks and far rumbles ([Weather](../systems/match-flow.md#weather)).
- **Day:** birds, cicadas in the heat, leaves in the wind.
- **Night:** crickets and frogs near water.
- **Water:** waves on beaches and rocks, the river, the crater lake's lapping.
- **Towns:** empty-town tones (a loose shutter, a creaking sign); no people.
- **Indoors:** a room tone that changes the tails of every sound inside.
