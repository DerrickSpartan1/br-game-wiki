## Real-world notes

3.35 kg; 18 in barrel; flash hider with ported sides that steady it during full auto; tritium sights; side-folding stock (telescopic on later models); the M762 version is the one PUBG players know

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native top Picatinny rail. Unique: Pallad wz. 74 40mm launcher (its own 40mm ammo) or GPBO-40 (NATO 40mm), rifle grenades launched with live ammo, bayonet, clip-on bipod, and Polish optics: CK-3 red dot, LKA-4 scope, CWL-1 scope with a built-in laser rangefinder, and PCS-5M/PCS-6 night sights (fits night maps). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 30 standard (20 also real); could share the M16's STANAG tiers
