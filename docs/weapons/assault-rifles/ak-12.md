## Real-world notes

3.5–3.7 kg; 922 mm long, 688 mm with the stock folded; 415 mm free-floating barrel; ambidextrous cocking handle and selector, with selector extensions for the trigger finger or the left thumb; the 2016 model has a 4-position telescoping side-folding stock, and the 2020 update an L-shaped 6-position polymer stock with an adjustable cheek piece and butt plate; mags have witness windows at 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 rounds and a glow-in-the-dark follower; rear sight set from 100 to 800 m; about 880–900 m/s; early field complaints were that it lost zero when the dust cover came off and went back on, and that the selector could be pushed down far enough to block the grip (flavor only); the 2023 model fixed handguard overheating and bent cocking handles

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Versions keep their real selectors, so some roll with the 2-round burst and some without it (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)).

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native rails on the receiver cover, the handguard (top, bottom, and sides), and the gas block. Unique: GP-34 / GP-25 40mm underbarrel launcher (front lug), 22mm NATO rifle grenades fired off the muzzle brake (2016–2021 models; the 2023 model's birdcage flash hider takes a quick-detach suppressor instead), and a bayonet. Mag tiers: 5.45 versions share the [AK-74](ak-74.md)'s tiers, 7.62×39 versions share the [AK-47 / AKM](ak-47-akm.md)'s, and the AK-19 is TBD (review later)

## Versions

9 versions after merging. The AK-12 and AK-15 production totals aren't split, so most of the 150,000+ are versions 1 and 6. The AK-308, AKV-721, RPL-20, AK-22, and the separate AM-17 went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list; the RPK-16 was already there. The cheaper AK-200 series is already in the [AK-74](ak-74.md) and [AK-47 / AKM](ak-47-akm.md) families.
