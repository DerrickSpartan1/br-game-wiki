## Real-world notes

3.1 kg, 960 mm long, 510 mm (20 in) barrel, 759 m/s. The Angle Eject of 1982 throws cases to the side so a scope can sit on top. Presidents Coolidge, Truman, and Eisenhower got the 1, 1.5, and 2 millionth rifles.

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 8 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). PUBG's Win94, a sniper-category lever gun, though PUBG's fired .45 ACP, which the real rifle never did. It loads one round at a time into its tube, and firing stops the reload ([Ammo](../../systems/ammo.md#how-ammo-and-reloading-work)).

## Attachments

DMR & sniper family attachments; adapter scope mount. Mag tiers: none, since it's tube-fed; ammo loops speed up reloads.
