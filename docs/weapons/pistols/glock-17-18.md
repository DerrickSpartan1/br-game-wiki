## Real-world notes

Polymer frame; no manual safety (three internal safeties); accessory rail from Gen 3; six generations. Main versions: Glock 17 (Gen 1 to 6), 17L (long slide), 17C (compensated), 17M, 18, 18C (compensated). The Glock 18 is only sold to military and police

## Versions

4 versions after merging, covering only the Glock 17 and 18; other models such as the 19 and 34 could become their own entries later. The compensated "C" models merge in, since a compensator is already a barrel attachment. Skins: generation looks (like the Gen 1 pebble finish), frame colors, and commemorative or agency engravings.
