## Real-world notes

4.4 kg; 20 in barrel; short-recoil action; overheated after about 300 rounds of continuous fire, so troops were told to fire mostly semi-auto; mags weren't interchangeable between guns, so each came with three that were reloaded with 5-round Arisaka stripper clips (kept in-game: it can top off its mag with stripper clips without swapping it); hard to clean and repair; some call it the first assault rifle

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; game-version scope mount. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 25 standard; 30 makeshift extended and quick; higher tiers TBD

## Versions

2 versions. 6.5×50mm Arisaka is new to the ammo pool, and Japan's Arisaka rifles use it too. The 1913 prototype's 6.5mm Fedorov would be an ultra-rare round. Quirk: both versions reload through the top with 5-round stripper clips, so you can top off the mag without swapping it. Overheating stays a flavor note.
