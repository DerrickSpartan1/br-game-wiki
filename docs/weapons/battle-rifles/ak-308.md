# AK-308

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Battle rifle |
| Cartridge | 7.62×51mm (all versions) |
| History | Russia; Kalashnikov Concern built it at the request of several foreign customers, aimed at armies that use both Russian and NATO-standard weapons; its first prototype was based on the AK-103 (a version of the [AK-47 / AKM](../assault-rifles/ak-47-akm.md)) with features from the [AK-12](../assault-rifles/ak-12.md); shown at the Army-2018 forum, where Pakistan's delegates were the most interested; a modernized version with the changes from the 2023 AK-12M1 was announced in June 2025, and the first batch for foreign customers was due in October 2025, with orders already coming in for 2026; export only, not meant for Russian service; buyers aren't named, though Pakistan, India, and Brazil are named as targets |
| Magazine (rounds) | 20-round polymer box of its own design (the 2025 mag has windows to show the round count); one source also lists 30-round mags for the 2018 rifle |
| Fire mode / rate | Semi and auto, about 700 RPM; Kalashnikov-style long-stroke gas piston with a rotating bolt; closed bolt, so no open-bolt delay |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Real-world 800 m aiming range (sights set from 100 to 800 m); in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

2018 rifle: 4.1 kg empty; 885–945 mm long depending on the stock, 690 mm folded; 415 mm chrome-lined barrel; stamped steel receiver with a removable top cover carrying a Picatinny rail; aluminum forend fixed to the receiver so it doesn't stress the barrel; front post on the gas block and an aperture rear sight; side-folding, telescoping four-position stock with a cleaning kit inside and storage in the pistol grip. 2025 rifle: 4.3 kg empty; 705 mm folded; about 760 m/s; longer free-floating handguard with side and bottom rails, a full-length top rail, a polymer folding stock with an RPK-style buttplate, a safety that blocks the bolt carrier and doubles as a dust cover, and a folding diopter sight with a bigger hole for low light and close range; no bolt hold-open

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It handles like a Kalashnikov but fires the NATO round, so it shares 7.62×51mm with the FAL, G3, and other battle rifles.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; Picatinny rail on the top cover (full length on the 2025 rifle, plus side and bottom rails on its handguard). Unique: the quick-detach suppressor, the bayonet, and the 2018 rifle's 40mm underbarrel grenade launcher (the 2025 rifle can't take one). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; higher tiers TBD
