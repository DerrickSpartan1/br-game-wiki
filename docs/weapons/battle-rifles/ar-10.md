## Real-world notes

3.29–4.05 kg; 1,050 mm long; 528 mm barrel; about 820 m/s; forged and machined aluminum receivers that hinge open like the [FN FAL](fn-fal.md)'s; the rotating bolt locks into a barrel extension, not the receiver; in Stoner's gas system, gas runs down a tube into the bolt carrier, the bolt acts as a fixed piston, and the carrier moves back like a cylinder; straight-line stock to cut muzzle climb on auto; fiberglass-reinforced stock with a foam core; the charging handle sits under the carry handle, which also holds the rear sight; dust cover on the ejection port like the [StG 44](../assault-rifles/stg-44.md)'s

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)), so the modern ArmaLite versions roll semi-auto only.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount on the carry handle of the original rifles, native rail on modern flat-top models. Unique: the A.I. 3× or 3.6× scope, the folding bipod (Transitional and Portuguese models), and rifle grenades (Portuguese grenade rifles, which got all-metal stocks). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; higher tiers TBD

## Versions

7 versions after merging. AR-10-pattern rifles by other makers are their own guns: the [SR-25 / M110](sr-25.md) and [HK417](hk417.md) have their own pages, and the rest went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list with the belt-fed AR-10 prototypes. Modern ArmaLite rifles also come in .243 Winchester, .260 Remington, 7mm-08 Remington, .338 Federal, and .300 Remington SAUM; those hunting rounds are left out for now, since no other gun in the game uses them.
