## Real-world notes

2.7 kg; 5.5 in barrel (6.5 in optional); the Super V sends the bolt downward behind the mag well, pushing recoil down instead of back to cut muzzle climb; the bore lines up with the shooter's hand, but the sights sit high above it; caliber swaps by changing the lower receiver; right-handed controls except the safety and selector; named after the kris dagger

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 8 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; native top rail; shares mags with the Glock family. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): .45: 13 standard, real 25-round MagEx as makeshift extended and quick, real 30-round MagEx2 as extended; 9mm: 33 standard, real 40-round MagEx2 as extended, middle tiers TBD

## Versions

8 versions after merging. The military SMG only came in .45 and 9mm; the other calibers were semi-auto civilian lowers and stay semi-auto. Skins: Cerakote colors (olive drab, Flat Dark Earth, Alpine white, Combat Gray). The SDP pistols moved to the 3rd slot as the Vector pistol. The KRISS KARD pistol went on the guns-to-add-later list. Decided: no mid-match caliber swaps (see Spawned version is fixed).
