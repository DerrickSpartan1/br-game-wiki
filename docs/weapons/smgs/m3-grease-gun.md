## Real-world notes

M3A1: 3.61 kg, 8 in barrel; cheap (about $20 in 1943); telescoping wire stock doubles as a cleaning rod and barrel wrench; the dust cover is its only safety, and it can fire if dropped; the single-feed mag is hard to load and jams in dirt; optional M9 flash hider

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): .45 versions: 30 standard; 35 makeshift extended and quick; 40 extended quick; 50 extended (game liberty). The 9mm versions use Sten mags, so they share the Sten's tiers. The suppressed version can't take barrel attachments
