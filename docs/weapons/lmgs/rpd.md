# RPD

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | LMG |
| Cartridge | 7.62×39mm (all versions) |
| History | USSR; Vasily Degtyaryov began work in 1943 and beat designs by Simonov and Sudayev; accepted as the RPD in 1944 and adopted in 1948, with large deliveries from 1953 and production until 1960; one of the first machine guns built for the new 7.62×39mm round; it supplemented the [DP-27](dp-27.md) and was pulled from most front-line Soviet units once the [RPK](rpk.md) arrived in the 1960s; copied in China (Type 56) and North Korea (Type 62); fought in Vietnam (including with US MACV-SOG teams), the Six-Day and Yom Kippur Wars, Afghanistan, the Iran–Iraq War, the Gulf War, Iraq, Syria, Yemen, and the Russo-Ukrainian War |

## Real-world notes

7.4 kg empty; 1,037 mm long; 520 mm barrel, which can't be removed; 735 m/s; empty belt segments drop away as it fires; chrome-lined chamber and bore; the return spring sits in the butt; the drum clogs with debris (flavor only); gunners were trained to fire short bursts to protect the barrel

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Light for a belt-fed gun, and it shares 7.62×39mm with the [AK-47 / AKM](../assault-rifles/ak-47-akm.md), [SKS](../battle-rifles/sks.md), and [RPK](rpk.md). The military version is auto only, with the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount (rails on the DS Arms Carbine). Unique: the 100-round belt drum and the bipod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 100-round belt standard; 125-round MACV-SOG belt as extended; quick tiers TBD
