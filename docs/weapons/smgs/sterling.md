## Real-world notes

2.7 kg; 7.7 in barrel; stock folds under the gun; grooved bolt scrapes out dirt and two recoil springs add reliability; light bolt makes it controllable and accurate in short bursts; its mag is often called one of the best ever designed; offset bayonet; used as the base for Star Wars stormtrooper blaster props

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 34 standard; 40 makeshift extended and quick; real twin-stacked 34-round mags as extended quick; the real 50-round Lanchester mag as extended (shared with the Sten). The .45 version uses Grease Gun mags and tiers. The suppressed version can't take barrel attachments

## Versions
