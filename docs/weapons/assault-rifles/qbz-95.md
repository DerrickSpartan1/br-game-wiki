# QBZ-95

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle (bullpup) |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 5.8×42mm (main) or 5.56×45mm |
| History | China; designed 1989–1991 by Duo Yingxian for Norinco; made since 1995 (about 3 million); first seen publicly at the 1997 Hong Kong handover; PLA service rifle that replaced the Type 81, now being replaced by the QBZ-191 |
| Magazine (rounds) | 30-round box (rocked in like an AK mag); 75-round drum also fits |
| Fire mode / rate | Semi and auto, about 650 RPM (selector also has a 3-round burst on some models); short-stroke piston with a linear striker inspired by the vz. 58; closed bolt, so no open-bolt delay |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Real-world 400 m; in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

3.4 kg; 18.2 in barrel; China claims its 5.8mm round shoots flatter and penetrates better than 5.56 or 5.45; charging handle hides under the carry handle; the original's safety sat awkwardly near the back of the gun, fixed on the QBZ-95-1, which also throws brass forward so lefties can use it

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; dovetail scope mount in the carry handle (rails on later mods). Unique: QLG-91B or QLG-10 35mm underbarrel launchers (35mm, not 40mm; the QLG-10 fires caseless grenades), 3× YMA95/QMK-171 scopes, rifle grenades fired with blanks, and the QNL-95 bayonet. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 30 standard; real 75-round drum as extended

## Versions

5 versions after merging. 5.8×42mm is new to the ammo pool. Triggers (game choice): the QBZ-97 gets semi, 3-round burst, and auto (as on the real 97A); the other military versions are semi/auto; the civilian version is semi-only. The QJB-95 and QJB-97 squad machine guns, the K12 PUMA bullpup shotgun, and the QBZ-191 went on the guns-to-add-later list.
