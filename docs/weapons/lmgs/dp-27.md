# DP-27

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | LMG |
| Cartridge | 7.62×54mmR (all versions) |
| History | USSR; designed by Vasily Degtyaryov in 1927 and issued from 1928 (some Western sources call it the DP-28); about 795,000 made from 1928 into the 1950s; Red Army troops called it the "record player" for its spinning pan; supplemented by the [RPD](rpd.md) in the 1950s and replaced by the [PK](pk.md) in the 1960s; fought from the Spanish Civil War and Winter War through WWII, Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan, Iraq, Syria, and the Russo-Ukrainian War; Finland used captured ones as the "Emma", and Germany issued captured ones to the Volkssturm; one of original PUBG's LMGs (as the DP-28) |

## Real-world notes

9.12 kg empty, 11.5 kg loaded; 1,270 mm long; 604 mm barrel; 840 m/s; the thin barrel heats quickly and needs a special wrench to change, and the recoil spring under the barrel could lose tension when hot (flavor only); no ordinary safety: a lever on the grip has to be held down to fire; the pan is driven by its own spring, unlike the Lewis gun's; pans were heavy, awkward to reload, and dented easily; cases eject downward; experimental SG-DP suppressors with low-power subsonic rounds were tried in 1941–42 and dropped

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 3 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Full-power 7.62×54mmR at a slow, steady rate. Auto only, with the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount. Unique: the bipod (detachable on the DP, fixed on the DPM) and the RP-46's 250-round belt box. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 47 standard; quick and extended tiers TBD
