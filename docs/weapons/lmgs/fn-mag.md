## Real-world notes

MAG 58: 10.85 kg, 1,255 mm long, 840 m/s; L7A2: 10.9 kg, 679 mm barrel; M240B: 12.25 kg, 627 mm barrel, 853 m/s; the receiver is steel plates riveted together; the M240 is rated at 26,000 mean rounds between failures; the barrel can burn without looking hot, and changing the gas setting means taking the barrel off; FN has offered over 130 configurations

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 6 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Heavy and steady. The military versions are auto only, with the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount (Picatinny rail on the M240B and later). Unique: the bipod and the tripod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 100-round belt standard; higher tiers TBD
