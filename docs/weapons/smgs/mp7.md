## Real-world notes

1.9 to 2.1 kg; 7.1 in barrel; full-length top rail plus side rails; folding foregrip and 3-position stock, so it fires one- or two-handed; low recoil; its steel-core round was built to beat body armor (balanced through damage, like 5.7mm)

## In-game traits

One roster entry with 1 version (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; native rails; its suppressor doesn't hurt accuracy or fire rate. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; real 30-round mag as makeshift extended and quick; real 40-round mag as extended quick; extended TBD
