## Real-world notes

4.38 kg (G3A3) to 4.7 kg (G3A4); 1,025 mm long; 450 mm barrel; about 800 m/s; the two-piece bolt's rollers delay the action at a 4:1 ratio; twelve flutes in the chamber ease extraction; built from pressed, stamped, and spot-welded steel, so the receiver and cocking tube can dent; the stock, handguard, and trigger group swap with push pins, a layout it shares with the [MP5](../smgs/mp5.md); heavy 50–55 N trigger pull for drop safety; the rotary drum rear sight has a V-notch for close range and apertures for 200, 300, and 400 m; the slotted flash hider takes a bayonet or rifle grenades; H&K also made a plastic-bullet training bolt and a .22 LR conversion kit

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Like H&K's other guns, its swappable trigger packs follow the fixed-trigger rule: each version gets one fixed trigger, mostly semi/auto, with burst mixed into a few (see [Rules](../../rules.md#fixed-triggers)). Versions keep their real fire modes, so the MSG90 and civilian versions roll semi-auto only.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; HK claw scope mount (same as the MP5 and [HK33](../assault-rifles/hk33.md)). Unique: the HK79 40mm underbarrel grenade launcher (the G3TGS setup), the Hensoldt Z24 4×24 scope, the bipod (wide handguard only), the bayonet, rifle grenades, and a suppressor. Mag tiers (all real mags): 20 standard; 30-round as makeshift extended and quick; 40-round as extended quick; 50- or 100-round drum as extended

## Versions

9 versions after merging. Fixed-stock (A3) and retractable-stock (A4) rifles, and the many license-built G3s, differ only in small details, so they merge. The G3TGS is an attachment setup (the HK79), not a version. The HK21 and HK11 machine guns, Myanmar's BA64, and the PSG1 sniper rifle went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list.
