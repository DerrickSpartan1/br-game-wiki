## Real-world notes

4.23 kg (heavy); 9.84 in barrel; the top-mounted mag uses gravity to help feeding, and the sights are offset to the side; the bolt is sealed in its own compartment, so it kept firing after being dipped in mud and sand when the Sten and Thompson stopped; quick barrel removal; nicknamed "Digger's Darling"

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 33 standard; 40 makeshift extended and quick; the real 60-round field-made horseshoe mag as extended quick; the real 72-round horseshoe mag as extended. Scopes need an offset game-version mount, since the mag sits on top

## Versions

5 versions after merging: the service gun plus four ultra-rare prototypes, one per cartridge. Skins: the Mk 1/42's wire stock, the Mk 1/43's wooden stock, and wartime camouflage paint.
