# IWI Tavor 7

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Battle rifle (bullpup) |
| Cartridge | 7.62×51mm (all versions) |
| History | Israel; designed by Israel Weapon Industries (IWI) from 2013 to 2017 as the 7.62mm member of the [Tavor](../assault-rifles/tavor.md) family, partly at customers' request; launched on 1 November 2017 and shown that month at Defense and Security Thailand, Milipol Paris, and Expodefensa Colombia; the US civilian model launched at SHOT Show 2018; pitched to military and police buyers, and a 2019 Jerusalem Post headline said IDF troops might get it, but none of the sources checked name a confirmed military user |

## Real-world notes

4.1 kg empty; 730 mm long with the 17 in (432 mm) barrel, short for a 7.62mm rifle thanks to the bullpup layout, and 806 mm with the 20 in barrel; the civilian 16.5 in rifle is 679.5 mm (26.75 in) and about 9 lb; hammer-forged, chrome-lined, free-floating barrel with a 1:12 twist, and the barrels are replaceable; the short-stroke piston moves less mass each shot, which one source says cuts recoil; fully ambidextrous: the safety, mag release, and bolt catch work like the X95's, and the ejection side and the non-reciprocating charging handle can switch sides (ejection needs partial disassembly: the bolt turns 180° and its cam pin flips); the semi-auto selector position sits at 45° rather than 90°; polymer body; the civilian rifle has no iron sights, a trigger of about 6 lb, and about 3 MOA accuracy, at $2,099 in 2020

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 3 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It's short for a full-power rifle, so it handles like a carbine. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)): the military versions are select-fire, and the civilian rifle rolls semi-auto only. Whether its gas-off setting becomes a manual mode on the selector key, like the [Mondragón rifle](mondragon.md)'s, is an [open question](../../planning/open-questions.md).

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; full-length Picatinny rail on top, M-LOK slots at 3 and 9 o'clock and a Picatinny rail at 6 o'clock on the handguard. Unique: the Meprolight MOR red dot (with built-in laser and IR pointer), the Meprolight M5 sight, and the swappable Tavor Talon trigger guard. Mag tiers (all real SR-25-pattern mags): 20 standard (10-round also real); 25-round as extended; quick tiers TBD
