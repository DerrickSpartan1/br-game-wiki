# Garrucha

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Pistol / 3rd slot |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: .320 and .380 revolver cartridges were most common in Brazil; also .22 LR and others |
| History | Civilian pocket pistol, common in Brazil and Argentina; made about 1930s to 1960s by Castelo, Rossi, and Lerap |
| Magazine (rounds) | 2 (double barrel) or 1 (single barrel) |
| Fire mode / rate | Break action; the Rossi has two triggers, one per barrel |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Not listed; in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

Derringer-like, usually with side-by-side barrels; rifled or smoothbore; associated with gauchos

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls a version. Plays as a pistol-like shotgun, not a funny gun

## Attachments

None

## Versions

The garrucha is a type of gun made by several makers rather than one model, so documented versions are thin. Only combinations with a source are listed.

| # | Version | What's different | Cartridge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Double-barrel, .320 | Side-by-side barrels; one of the two most popular Brazilian chamberings | .320 Revolver |
| 2 | Double-barrel, .380 | Side-by-side barrels; the other most popular Brazilian chambering | .380 Revolver (not .380 ACP) |
| 3 | Rossi double-barrel (1950s to 60s) | 2.75 in side-by-side barrels, two triggers, flip-up barrel lever, brass bead front sight | .22 LR |
| 4 | Single-barrel | One barrel | Not documented |

Still to research: garruchas were also chambered for .32 S&W, .38 S&W, .22 Short, .22 Long, and .32, 8mm, and 9mm Flobert, but which makers and barrel setups used them isn't documented.
