## Real-world notes

2.54 kg; 8.9 in barrel; roller-delayed action gives lower recoil than straight blowback; drum rear sight; claw mount takes optics, lights, and lasers; threaded muzzle for suppressors; can launch 22mm rifle grenades with an adapter

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; native claw mount for scopes. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 9mm: 30 standard; real 40-round mag as makeshift extended and quick; real 50-round mag or drum as extended quick; real 100-round Beta C-Mag as extended. The MP5SD can't take barrel attachments; the MP5K takes no muzzle devices but can take a folding stock (from the K-PDW)

## Versions

9 versions after merging. Stocks (fixed, retractable, none) merge into each version. Triggers are fixed per version (game choice, not historical): the MP5, Operational Briefcase, MP5/40, MP5/45, and .22 use the standard semi/auto trigger; the MP5K gets the 4-position trigger (semi, 3-round burst, auto); the MP5SD gets the burst pack (semi and 3-round burst); the MP5/10 keeps its 2-round burst (semi, 2-round burst, auto); the semi-auto carbine is semi-only. Civilian semi-auto pistols moved to the 3rd slot as the MP5 pistol. Left out: the never-released 6.5×25mm kit and HK's SMG I, SMG II, and MP2000 prototypes.
