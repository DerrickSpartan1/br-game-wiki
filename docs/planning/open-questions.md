# Open questions

Everything still undecided or still to look up. When one is settled, it moves to the right page and gets a line in the [decision log](decisions.md).

## Balance

- **Damage and in-game range:** TBD on every gun page. [TTK](../systems/ttk.md) needs more discussion first.
- **Mag tiers:** every gun's current numbers are best guesses, to review later ([Attachments](../systems/attachments.md)).
- **AN-94:** crate-only or a very rare world spawn ([AN-94](../weapons/assault-rifles/an-94.md)).
- **SCAR-L and SCAR-H:** whether they share one mastery track and cosmetics, since they were built as one family with the same ergonomics, though the SCAR-H will sit in battle rifles ([FN SCAR-L](../weapons/assault-rifles/scar-l.md)).
- **Crate weapons:** how a gun earns a crate spot is undecided, and crate balancing comes later. One idea: a crate gun could be a normal gun that drops fully kitted with attachments ([Crate weapons](../weapons/crate/index.md)).
- **M14 selector:** the standard M14 is select-fire for now, though most US rifles were issued locked to semi ([M14](../weapons/battle-rifles/m14.md)).
- **AS Val and VSS:** whether they share one mastery track and cosmetics. They're twins from the same designers, with the same action and magazines, but they sit in different categories ([VSS Vintorez](../weapons/battle-rifles/vss.md)).
- **Map caps:** the per-map max for throwables and crate weapons isn't set ([Weapon pool](../systems/weapon-pool.md)).
- **Wear:** whether some items wear out. Current ideas: the makeshift suppressor and the StG 44's Krummlauf curved barrel (about 300 rounds in real life). Guns themselves never jam or overheat.

## Systems

- **In-game gun names:** whether the game uses real names, PUBG-style tweaks (M416, SLR, Mk14), or fictional codenames, to avoid trademark trouble. The wiki keeps real names either way.
- **Gas cut-offs:** whether other guns with a real gas cut-off (the FAL's gas plug, and the grenade cut-offs on the MAS-49/56, M59/66, and [BM 59](../weapons/battle-rifles/bm-59.md)) also get the Mondragón's manual mode on the selector key.
- **Maps:** not decided yet ([Maps](../systems/maps.md)).
- **Server hosting:** not decided ([Servers](../systems/servers.md)).
- **Scrap types** for in-match crafting ([Crafting](../systems/crafting.md)).
- **Season challenges:** how weapon-specific goals work when that gun may not roll for a while ([Seasons](../systems/seasons.md)).
- **Rifle grenade launchers:** how they're looted: crate-only, rare world loot, or something else ([Attachments](../systems/attachments.md)).
- **Wire fences:** a possibility, not decided: drive through them, or cut a quiet gap with wire cutters (a melee item) or the Galil ARM's bipod ([Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md)).
- **Briefcase spawns:** an idea, not decided. The AS Val and OTs-14 Groza came in real carry cases, so they could spawn as a briefcase find.
