# AS Val

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Assault rifle (integrally suppressed) |
| Cartridge | 9×39mm (subsonic) |
| History | USSR; designed by Pyotr Serdyukov and Vladimir Krasnikov at TsNIITochMash (1985), adopted 1987, made at Tula; built for Spetsnaz and KGB/GRU covert work; used in both Chechen wars, Georgia 2008, and Ukraine |
| Magazine (rounds) | 20-round box (10-round VSS and 30-round SR-3M mags also fit); ribbed so they can't be confused with AK mags |
| Fire mode / rate | Semi and full auto, about 800–900 RPM; selector sits behind the trigger; AK-style long-stroke piston, closed bolt, so no open-bolt delay |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Real-world 400 m by day, 300 m at night; in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

2.5 kg; 7.9 in barrel inside a built-in two-chamber suppressor, so it's always suppressed; even from close by, it doesn't sound like a rifle (big for the sound-focused design); subsonic bullet (280–310 m/s), so heavy drop and slow travel at range; iron sights are built onto the suppressor; side-folding stock; breaks down into a briefcase (could be a fun crate/briefcase spawn)

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments, but no muzzle or suppressor slot on the Val (suppressor is built in); side dovetail scope mount (rails on the modernized ASM). Unique: PSO-1 scope and NSPU-3 night sight (fits night maps). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; 25 makeshift extended and quick; 28 extended quick; real 30-round SR-3M mag as extended

## Versions

2 versions after merging. 9×39mm is now shared with the AK-74 family's 9×39 versions. The VSS Vintorez (the Val's sniper twin), its modernized VSSM, and the civilian KO VSS went on the guns-to-add-later list for the DMR or sniper category.
