## Real-world notes

M7: 3.80 kg (8.38 lb), or 4.46 kg (9.84 lb) with its suppressor; about 914 mm long with the suppressor; 13 in barrel; about 3,000 ft/s (915 m/s); built to carry its SIG SLX suppressor full-time, since the high-pressure round has magnum-level blast and strong recoil; the 6.8mm round uses a hybrid case with brass walls and a steel or aluminum-alloy base, about 20–30% lighter per empty case; seven 20-round mags (140 rounds) weigh 9.8 lb, against 210 rounds and 7.4 lb for an M4A1 load; controls much like the M4's, with a non-reciprocating folding charging handle on the left plus the rear T-handle; the shortened 2025 SLX suppressor with a thermal shield hides its glow from night and thermal sights for about 100 rounds, against about 40 before; the M7 and M8 share a serialized lower, and SIG says swapping the barrel and handguard takes under five minutes. M8: 3.3 kg (7.3 lb) empty, 4.0 kg (8.8 lb) with suppressor and heat shield; fixed stock, stiffer handguard, softer butt pad. Civilian rifles: 13 in barrel at 8.38 lb with a mag, or 16 in at 9.2 lb; 1:7 twist; two-stage match trigger; about $4,000

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It's the first gun in 6.8×51mm, so for now its ammo feeds only this gun; the M250 machine gun would share it later. The M7 and M8 spawn with their SLX suppressor, since they're built to carry it full-time. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)): the M7 is select-fire, the civilian versions roll semi-auto only, and the M8 is TBD.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; full-length Picatinny rail on top and a free-floating M-LOK handguard. Unique: the SIG SLX suppressor (and its shortened 2025 version with a thermal shield) and the Army's M157 fire control optic. Mag tiers (all real mags): 20 standard; 25-round as extended; quick tiers TBD
