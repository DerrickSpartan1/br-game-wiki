## Real-world notes

RPK: 4.8 kg empty, 1,040 mm long, 590 mm barrel; RPK-74: 4.7 kg, 1,060 mm, 590 mm barrel, 960 m/s; RPK-16: 6 kg combat weight, 1,076 mm, 550 mm (LMG) or 370 mm (rifle) barrel; a thicker receiver than the AKM (1.5 mm against 1.0 mm) and a longer, heavier barrel that holds its accuracy better when hot, but it's fixed and can't be swapped in the field; club-foot stock like the RPD's; a rear sight with its own windage knob; the RPK-16 has interchangeable barrels and a detachable suppressor

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The RPK, RPK-74, and RPK-16 are one LMG family here, split by cartridge, while the [AKM](../assault-rifles/ak-47-akm.md) and [AK-74](../assault-rifles/ak-74.md) stay separate assault rifle families. It handles like a heavy, steady AK rather than a belt-fed gun, with semi and auto on every version. It shares ammo with the AK families.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; side-rail scope mount on night-sight (N) versions and the RPK-74M, Picatinny rails on the RPK-16. Unique: the 75-round drum, the RPK-16's 95-round drum and detachable suppressor, and the NSP-3 and NSPU night sights. Mag tiers (all real mags, by cartridge): 7.62mm uses a 40 standard, a 30-round AKM mag as makeshift quick, and the 75-round drum as extended; 5.45mm uses a 45 standard, a 30-round AK-74 mag as makeshift quick, and the 95-round drum as extended; extended quick TBD
