## Real-world notes

Ambidextrous thumb safety; tritium sights. Versions: M17 full-size (4.7 in barrel, 834 g) and M18 compact (3.9 in barrel, 737 g); the commercial P320-M17 and P320-M18 merge in (no tamper-resistant screws, with or without a manual safety). Skins: the four ceremonial Tomb Sentinel M17s (aluminum frames, wood grips from USS Olympia deck planks), the M17 Commemorative (5,000 made to original Army spec with brown controls), and black or coyote brown finishes

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions. M17: more accurate and better at longer range (longer barrel and sight radius). M18: lighter, with a quicker draw and ADS, but a bit more spread at range

## Attachments

Red dot (native slide cut), light (rail), pistol/SMG suppressor and barrel attachments, pistol/SMG mags
