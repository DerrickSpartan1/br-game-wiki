## Real-world notes

2.61 kg with a 9 in barrel, 2.72 kg with 11.5 in, 3.6 kg with 16 in; 730–908 mm long with the stock open; piston system based on SIG's MPX submachine gun; dual recoil springs, which SIG says soften recoil; folding stock in four configurations; in real life the barrel swaps in seconds to another length or caliber; 5.56 and .300 Blackout use the same STANAG mags; the upper fits AR-15 / M16 lowers with an adapter; mostly ambidextrous controls, including the charging handle, though the bolt release only went ambidextrous on the SPEAR LT; tapered crown for direct-thread suppressors; KeyMod handguard on the first generation, M-LOK from the VIRTUS on; full-length top rail

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Like the [FN SCAR-L](scar-l.md), its real barrel and caliber swaps don't happen mid-match: each barrel length and caliber is its own version, so the spawned version is the gun you keep. The 7.62×39 version and the civilian models are semi-auto only (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)).

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; native top rail, plus a KeyMod or M-LOK handguard. Unique: none found. Mag tiers: 5.56 and .300 Blackout versions share the [M16](m16-m4.md)'s STANAG tiers; 7.62×39 TBD (review later)

## Versions

9 versions after merging, split by caliber, barrel length, and whether it's select-fire. No production numbers were found, and the police and military orders (Berlin 460, Schleswig-Holstein 531, Royal Marines 1,500, and others) aren't split by barrel or caliber. The MCX-SPEAR / M7, the MCX-MR, and the MPX went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list.
