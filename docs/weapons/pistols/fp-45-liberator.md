## Real-world notes

Smoothbore 4 in barrel, so bullets tumble past short range; 450 g; 250 m/s; about 1 million made by GM in roughly 11 weeks at $2.10 each; spent case pushed out with a wooden dowel; 10 spare rounds stored in the grip

## In-game traits

Higher damage, single shot, slow reload. Comes pre-loaded when picked up: one round in the chamber plus 10 stored in the grip, which don't take backpack space
