**Pistol / 3rd-slot version:** [SIG P556](../pistols/sig-p556.md)

## Real-world notes

4.1 kg; 20.8 in barrel; famously accurate (every rifle test-fired at the factory's underground 300 m range); built-in bipod that folds into the handguard; side-folding skeleton stock; tritium night inserts in the sights (fits night maps); trigger guard folds aside for gloves; locks open on empty

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; quick-detach rail adapters (built-in rail on some versions). Unique: GL 5040/5140 40mm underbarrel launcher (GL 5340 on the compact 552/553), Kern 4×24 scope with bullet-drop dial, rifle grenades fired with live ammo off the flash hider, and the Stgw 90 bayonet made by Victorinox (the Swiss Army knife company). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 20 standard; real 30-round mag as extended; the clip-together studs could be the quick or extended-quick tier

## Versions

7 versions after merging. The military versions keep the real semi, 3-round burst, and auto selector. The SIG P556 pistol moved to the 3rd slot. The SG 550-1 Sniper, the SG 750/751 full-power rifles, the SG 540 family, and the earlier SG 510 / Stgw 57 went on the guns-to-add-later list.
