## Real-world notes

4.18 kg; 7.9 in barrel; fires from an open bolt and could go off if the stock was knocked with the bolt forward; often called the first submachine gun (debated); used the same TM 08 snail drum as the Luger LP08; over 30,000 MP 18s made, but only about 4,000 likely saw combat

## In-game traits

Full auto only on every version; one roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; the snail drum is its extended mag (the same TM 08 drum as the Luger LP08)

## Versions

7 versions after merging, one per cartridge. Drum versus box feeds merge because mags are covered by attachments; the MP 28/II merges in too, and every version is full auto only. Skins: the Pieper-assembled Belgian guns, Chinese copies with bottom-mounted mags, and Britain's Lanchester copy.
