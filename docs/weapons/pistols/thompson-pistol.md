## Real-world notes

No stock; 13 in barrel with an aluminum receiver (1927A5) or 10.5 in barrel with a steel receiver (TA5)

## In-game traits

Merges the 1927A5 and TA5

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; same drum tiers as the Thompson (100-round extended, 50-round extended quick)
