## Real-world notes

3.07 kg (AK-74M 3.4 kg); 16.3 in barrel; about half the AKM's recoil and a flatter shot (400 m battle zero vs. 300 m); more accurate than the AKM; its big muzzle brake cuts recoil a lot but blasts gas sideways, so it's loud for anyone next to you (good sound-design hook); 5.45 is light, so a soldier carries about 540 rounds vs. 360 for the AKM; AK-74 mags have a rib so they can't go into a 7.62 AK

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; side-rail scope mount on N models and the AK-74M, adapter mount otherwise. Unique: GP-25/GP-30/GP-34 40mm underbarrel launchers (not on the AKS-74U), the suppressed 30mm BS-1 launcher (AKS-74U only), 6H5 bayonet with a wire cutter (could tie into wire fences), and the 1P29 and 1P78 Kashtan scopes with built-in rangefinder reticles. Mag tiers (all real mags): 30 standard; 45-round RPK-74 mag as makeshift extended and quick; 60-round casket as extended quick; 96-round drum as extended

## Versions

7 versions after merging. 9×39mm is new to the ammo pool (the AS Val will share it). Plum and laminate furniture are skins. The AK-103, AK-104, AK-203, and AK-204 stay in the AK-47 / AKM family since they're 7.62×39. The [AK-12](ak-12.md), [AN-94](an-94.md), and [OTs-14 Groza](ots-14-groza.md) keep their own pages; the RPK-74 and RPK-16 went on the guns-to-add-later list. The balanced-recoil AK-107 and AK-108 fold in here as rare versions so they don't outshine the base gun; the 7.62×39 AK-109 went to the AK-47 / AKM family.
