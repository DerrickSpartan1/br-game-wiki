## Real-world notes

2.6 kg, 3.4 kg with the scope; 894 mm long; 200 mm barrel inside a built-in two-chamber suppressor with a vented barrel and mesh screens, so it's always suppressed and sounds nothing like a rifle; subsonic at 280–310 m/s, so its heavy bullets drop hard and travel slowly at range; the barrel is tuned for the SP-5 round's 250-grain bullet; iron sights built onto the suppressor; Kalashnikov-style safety with the fire selector behind the trigger; breaks down into an aluminum briefcase along with its PSO-1-1 scope and night sight; it can also mount the [SVD](svd.md)'s PSO-1

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 3 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The military versions spawn with their scope. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)), so the VSS and VSSM keep auto, and the civilian KO VSS rolls semi-auto only.

## Attachments

DMR/sniper family attachments, but no muzzle or suppressor slot, since the suppressor is built in; side dovetail scope mount (Picatinny rail on the VSSM and KO VSS). Unique: the PSO-1-1 (1P43) scope, the NSPU-3 night sight (fits night maps), and the VSSM's 1P86 1×/4× scope. Mag tiers (all real mags, shared with the AS Val): 10 standard; 20-round Val mag as makeshift extended and quick; 30-round SR-3M mag as extended; extended quick TBD
