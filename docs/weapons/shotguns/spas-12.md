## Real-world notes

4.4 kg; about 37,000 made; folding stock with a butt hook for one-handed firing; magazine cut-off lets you chamber a different shell; threaded barrel for chokes and a factory shot diverter; France used a 44 mm grenade launcher on it

## In-game traits

Switches between semi-auto and pump like the real gun; one roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Tube extension attachment (real extension tubes held 5 to 8 shells); other attachments TBD

## Versions

2 versions after merging. Its single-mode sister guns merge in, following the AA-12 precedent. Skins: the original wooden detachable stock, the folding metal stock with hook, and the fixed synthetic "Sporting Purpose" stock.
