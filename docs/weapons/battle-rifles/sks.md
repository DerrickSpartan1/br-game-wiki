## Real-world notes

3.85 kg; about 1,021 mm long with the bayonet folded; 521 mm barrel, chrome-lined on most; about 735 m/s; open rear sight marked 100–1,000 m, plus a fixed 300 m battle setting; folding bayonet under the barrel, spike or blade depending on maker and year; the bolt locks open on an empty mag; cleaning kit in a trapdoor in the stock; loose tolerances to keep working when dirty; since it loads from stripper clips, it can be topped off at any time, unlike the [M1 Garand](m1-garand.md)'s en bloc clip; a stuck floating firing pin could slam-fire in real life (flavor only)

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below).

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount, except the marksman version, which spawns with its scope. Unique: the folding blade or spike bayonet, the M59/66's built-in 22mm rifle grenade launcher with its fold-up ladder sight, and the M59/66A1's glowing night sights. Mag tiers: none on the fixed-mag versions (10-round internal mag); the AK-mag versions share the [AK-47 / AKM](../assault-rifles/ak-47-akm.md)'s tiers

## Versions

7 versions after merging. The Soviet SKS-45 and the many foreign copies differ only in small details (steel, stamped parts, bayonet type, stock), so they merge into one version. The Chinese Type 63 assault rifle and the PTRS-41 anti-tank rifle went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list.
