## Real-world notes

4.5 to 4.9 kg; 10.5 in barrel; Cutts compensator and finned barrel on early models; first gun marketed as a "submachine gun"; about 1.75 million made; soldiers found the drums heavy, rattly, and slow to load

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 13 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

Pistol/SMG family attachments; the 100-round drum is the extended mag and the 50-round drum the extended quick mag, on every version (including the M1 and M1A1, which couldn't take drums in real life)

## Versions

13 versions after merging. Every version can take drums in-game. The Thompson pistol moved to the pistol / 3rd slot as its own entry. Civilian semi-auto replicas (1927A1, Commando, TM1, 1927A-1C Lightweight Deluxe, 1928A1 LTD) and foreign copies (Swedish m/40, Chinese Taiyuan Arsenal and Type 36, Egyptian and Turkish copies) become skins. Calibers that were only offered, never documented as made, are left out.
