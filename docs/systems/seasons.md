# Seasons

Season 1 is included with the base game.

Seasons have no time limits. Players choose which season to work on, like Halo, and nothing is time-gated.

- **Season pass:** new skins and unlocks; cosmetic bling.
- **New weapons:** each season adds weapons to the pool, and nobody has to unlock them to use them.
- **Challenges:** dailies are vague, like damage with ARs. Bigger goals target a gun family, not one version: kills with the AK family count any AK version, and the same goes for the M4 or 1911 family (owner, 2026-11-22). A family shows up in more matches than any one version does.
- **Quests (later):** quests and other goals for players come later.
