# Open questions

Everything still undecided or still to look up. When one is settled, it moves to the right page and gets a line in the [decision log](decisions.md).

## Balance

- **In-game range:** TBD on every gun page. Damage has a first pass on every version ([Starting damage](../systems/starting-damage.md)), to tune in play testing.
- **CS gas rounds:** whether launchers get tear gas later, which would need a gas effect of its own ([Grenade launchers](../systems/attachments.md#ground-rounds-smoke-and-other-non-lethal-rounds)).
- **Handling numbers:** a first pass per category, from PUBG's numbers, is on [Handling](../systems/handling.md). ADS times aren't in PUBG's data, so those are our picks; all of it gets tuned in play testing.
- **Mag tiers:** every gun's current numbers are best guesses, to review later ([Attachments](../systems/attachments.md)).
- **SCAR-L and SCAR-H:** whether they share one mastery track and cosmetics, since they were built as one family with the same ergonomics, though the SCAR-H sits in battle rifles & DMRs ([FN SCAR-L](../weapons/assault-rifles/scar-l.md), [FN SCAR-H](../weapons/battle-rifles/scar-h.md)).
- **Crate weapons:** the crate rule covers anti-vehicle and heavy snipers, rocket launchers, lethal grenade launchers and launcher rounds, and belt-fed guns too fast for the ground, but other guns' spots are undecided, and crate balancing comes later (the MG 42 / MG3 family could earn back some of its cut damage then). Every crate already comes with attachments that fit its gun; one idea is that a crate gun could also be a normal gun dropped fully kitted ([Crate weapons](../weapons/crate/index.md)).
- **M14 selector:** the standard M14 is select-fire for now, though most US rifles were issued locked to semi ([M14](../weapons/battle-rifles/m14.md)).
- **AS Val and VSS:** whether they share one mastery track and cosmetics. They're twins from the same designers, with the same action and magazines, but they sit in different categories ([VSS Vintorez](../weapons/battle-rifles/vss.md)). One idea: twin guns in different categories share one mastery track while keeping separate roster entries ([Skins and mastery](../systems/skins.md#weapon-mastery)).
- **Wear:** whether some items wear out. Current ideas: the makeshift suppressor and the StG 44's Krummlauf curved barrel (about 300 rounds in real life). Guns themselves never jam or overheat.

## Systems

- **LMG handling:** how much slower LMGs move, aim, and reload belts; the direction (slowest ADS and movement, steady only on a bipod, slow belt reloads) is on [Roles by category](../systems/ttk.md#roles-by-category). Bipods are decided ([Attachments](../systems/attachments.md#bipods)), and reloads already top off like PUBG ([Ammo](../systems/ammo.md#how-ammo-and-reloading-work), [LMGs](../weapons/lmgs/index.md)).
- **Shields:** the first pass is approved; whether the flash shield really uses the class slot ([Shields](../systems/class-items.md#shields)).
- **LMG family splits:** the RPK, RPK-74, and RPK-16 share one LMG page, split by cartridge, while the AKM and AK-74 are separate rifle families; the Bren and ZB vz. 26 also share one page, since the Bren is a licensed ZB ([RPK](../weapons/lmgs/rpk.md), [Bren / ZB vz. 26](../weapons/lmgs/bren.md)).
- **Game names:** our words for the red zone, blue zone, and boost bar ([Game names](game-names.md), [PUBG look and feel](legal-review.md#pubg-look-and-feel)).
- **Gas cut-offs:** whether other guns with a real gas cut-off (the FAL's gas plug, and the grenade cut-offs on the MAS-49/56, M59/66, and [BM 59](../weapons/battle-rifles/bm-59.md), and the [IWI Tavor 7](../weapons/battle-rifles/tavor-7.md)'s gas-off setting) also get the Mondragón's manual mode on the selector key.
- **Match flow numbers:** plane altitude and speed, care packages per match, and each red zone's size, warning, and length ([Match flow](../systems/match-flow.md)).
- **Server hosting:** not decided ([Servers](../systems/servers.md)).
- **Workbenches and scrap:** test the first-pass costs, craft times, bench count, and scrap per match; what scrap buys out of game ([Crafting](../systems/crafting.md#workbenches)).
- **Victory line:** the game's own line for a win ([Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md#match-setup)).
- **Suppression:** whether near misses blur or shake your view, and how much ([Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md#sound)).
- **Bodies:** what happens to a body once nobody can revive it but the player is still watching, and whether a looted body can still be revived ([Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md#knockdowns-revives)).
- **Red zone forms:** which new forms to add; ideas so far are firebombing, a creeping barrage, and EMP ([Match flow](../systems/match-flow.md#red-zones)).
- **Throwable weights:** PUBG's weights for the sticky bomb, C4, decoy, and blue zone grenade ([Throwables](../systems/weapon-pool.md#throwables)).
- **Vehicles per map:** which vehicles each map gets ([Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md#vehicles-and-water)).
- **Melee and tools:** the melee, pan, and breaching tool numbers look good to the owner but need play testing; check the numbers for throwing knives and nailing doors shut, and mortars' range and blast; whether the door wedge is still needed now that doors can be nailed, where wooden boxes for the small crowbar show up, and what the screwdriver does ([Melee](../systems/gameplay.md#melee), [Class items](../systems/class-items.md#pans), [Breaching tools](../systems/class-items.md#breaching-tools), [Nailing doors shut](../systems/gameplay.md#nailing-doors-shut)).
- **Characters:** whether hair, eye, and skin colors are a full color picker or a set of grounded colors; which options are free and which unlock; and whether players can save several characters ([Your character](../systems/skins.md#your-character)).
- **PvE:** robot guards in vaults are the first form ([Robot guards](../systems/match-flow.md#robot-guards-idea)); what else PvE could be, and the guards' numbers.
- **New class items:** how to make the signal jammer worth the slot, and how gas, the gas trap, and the gas mask balance ([More class items](../systems/class-items.md#more-class-items)).
- **Taking things out of a match:** how scrap, quest items, and skin-crafting items come out with you, and what you keep when you die or win ([Crafting](../systems/crafting.md)).
- **Dragon's breath fires:** how long its ground fires burn ([Crate shotgun ammo](../systems/starting-damage.md#crate-shotgun-ammo)).
- **Comeback duel:** loadouts, the arena's layout, and the duel timer ([Comeback fight](../systems/gameplay.md#comeback-fight)).
- **Gun family names:** whether gun cards show a family game name or only the version's ([Game names](game-names.md)).
- **Krepost:** how players get up the rock; waiting on reference photos ([Maps](../systems/maps.md#layout-coordinates)).
- **Waist and thigh armor:** a separate piece, part of vest combos, or both, and its tiers and numbers ([Waist and thigh armor](../systems/armor.md#waist-and-thigh-armor-idea)).
- **Animals:** how birds and other animals give players away ([Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md#tracks-and-animals)).
- **Match details:** radar station cost and spots, how many care packages drop, and how wanted-player contracts and moving loot work ([Match flow](../systems/match-flow.md)).
- **Season challenges:** how weapon-specific goals work when that gun may not roll for a while ([Seasons](../systems/seasons.md)).
- **Wire fences:** a possibility, not decided: drive through them, or cut a quiet gap with the built-in wire cutters or the Galil ARM's bipod ([Gameplay](../systems/gameplay.md#built-in-tools)).
- **Briefcase spawns:** an idea, not decided. The AS Val and OTs-14 Groza came in real carry cases, so they could spawn as a briefcase find.
