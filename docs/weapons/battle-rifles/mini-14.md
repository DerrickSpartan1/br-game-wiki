## Real-world notes

Light at 2.9 kg; 965 mm long; barrels from 330 to 559 mm, mostly 409 or 470 mm; about 990 m/s; investment-cast, heat-treated receiver; a fixed-piston gas system Ruger calls self-cleaning; early rifles had a poor reputation for accuracy, and a 2003 overhaul (the "580-series") plus a heavier tapered barrel around 2007–2008 brought it to about 2 MOA; the Ranch Rifle has integral scope bases and comes with Ruger scope rings

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 10 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It plays as a light, fast-handling marksman rifle, like PUBG's Mini14. Versions keep their real fire modes (see [Rules](../../rules.md#versions)): the AC-556 versions keep their real burst and auto, and the UK version is a straight-pull manual rifle.

## Attachments

DMR/sniper family attachments; integral scope bases (Picatinny rail on current models), adapter scope mount on the original. Unique: Ruger's factory scope rings, the AC-556's flash suppressor that launches tear-gas and smoke grenades, the GB's bayonet, and a stripper clip guide (Cogburn Arsenal). Mag tiers (all real mags, counting aftermarket ones): 20 standard; 30-round as makeshift extended and quick; 40-round as extended quick; 90-round drum as extended
