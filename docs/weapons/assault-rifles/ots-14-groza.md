## Real-world notes

A bullpup AKS-74U: shares about three-quarters of its parts; 2.74 kg as a carbine, about 3.97 kg with the grenade launcher; balanced enough to fire one-handed like a pistol, but criticized as heavy, with the AK action awkwardly close to your face; came in an aluminum case with all its parts (another briefcase-spawn candidate)

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 4 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; scope mount on the carry handle plus a night-sight dovetail. Unique: an integrated 40mm grenade launcher grip module (one trigger fires either the rifle or the launcher, picked with a selector switch; uses GP-25/GP-30 grenades), its own quick-detach suppressor, and a vertical foregrip. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 9×39 starts at 20; 7.62×39 uses the AK's tiers (30/40/50/75)

## Versions

4 versions after merging. Its real configurations (foregrip rifle, suppressor-ready carbine, scoped "sniper" kit, grenade launcher) are just attachment setups, so they merge into each caliber version. The OTs-12 Tiss it grew out of is already a 9×39 version in the AK-74 family.
