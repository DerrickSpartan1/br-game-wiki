## Real-world notes

8.5 in barrel; 16.7 in long; 4.5 lb (about 2 kg); pistol grip and no stock; adjustable flip-up sights; Picatinny top rail and M-LOK handguard; built to look like the HK416, but it works by simple blowback rather than a gas piston

## In-game traits

Moved to the 3rd slot like the other long-gun pistols
