# QBU-88

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | DMR (bullpup) |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 5.8×42mm (main) or 5.56×45mm |
| History | China; made by Norinco as the Type 88 and one of the first rifles of China's new generation of small arms, alongside the [QBZ-95](../assault-rifles/qbz-95.md); sources date its adoption to the late 1980s or 1997; a designated marksman rifle rather than a true sniper rifle, giving squads aimed fire past assault rifle range; used by the People's Liberation Army, the People's Armed Police, and Chinese police; being replaced by the QBU-191 (already gone in the Tibet Military District) and, in the PLA Navy Marine Corps, by the CS/LR4; one of original PUBG's DMRs |
| Magazine (rounds) | 10-round detachable box (its own design; STANAG mags only fit after modification) |
| Fire mode / rate | Semi-auto; short-stroke gas piston above the barrel with a three-lug rotating bolt, closed bolt, so no open-bolt delay |
| Damage | TBD |
| Effective range | Real-world 500–1,000 m (sources differ: one gives up to 800 m); in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

4.1 kg; 920 mm long, short for its 620–640 mm match-grade, hammer-forged barrel (sources differ) thanks to the bullpup layout; steel receiver inside a polymer housing; safety at the bottom of the receiver just behind the magazine; charging handle on the right; long birdcage flash hider; built around a heavier, streamlined steel-core 5.8mm sniper load with a faster rifling twist than the QBZ-95, but it also fires standard QBZ-95 ammo; folding diopter iron sights; a quick-detach bipod clamps to the barrel

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It shares the QBZ-95's 5.8×42mm ammo, so the two help each other through the cartridge weighting.

## Attachments

DMR/sniper family attachments; short proprietary dovetail rail on the receiver. Unique: the 3–9×40 military scope with a quick-release mount, the 6–24×44 police and counter-terrorism scope (2008), the quick-detach bipod, a night sight, and the aftermarket PEAK 88 upper with a full-length Picatinny rail. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 10 standard; higher tiers TBD

## Versions

2 versions after merging. The QBU-191 marksman rifle (built on the QBZ-191) and the QBU-141 and CS/LR4 bolt-action sniper rifles are separate guns; they went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list.
