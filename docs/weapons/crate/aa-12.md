## Real-world notes

5.2 kg; 18 in barrel; fires from an open bolt; recoil system leaves about 10% of normal 12-gauge recoil; reportedly fires after being submerged; built to need little cleaning; rail available for optics

## In-game traits

A true monster of a crate weapon; one roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Buckshot hits 22 per pellet (198 a shell) at 300 RPM, so two shells kill through a tier 4 vest in 0.2 s up close. The ammo type rolled for the match sets its job ([Crate shotgun ammo](../../systems/starting-damage.md#crate-shotgun-ammo)):

- **Buckshot:** the close-range one-shot.
- **Slugs:** reach to about 100 m.
- **FRAG-12:** a small blast on impact that ignores armor, out to about 175 m; it doesn't arm inside 3 m. Two direct hits kill through a tier 4 vest.
- **Dragon's breath:** burning pellets out to 20 m that set targets on fire through armor and stop them from aiming down sights.
