## Real-world notes

Heavy: 4.98 kg loaded with the SUSAT; 20.4 in barrel in a bullpup body, so high velocity and long fragmentation range; right-handed only (ejection port and reciprocating cocking handle on the right), which matters if the game ever adds shoulder swapping; the A1 was notoriously unreliable (mag release caught on clothing, top cover had to be taped shut, safety plunger swelled when wet), while the A2 became one of the most reliable rifles of its type

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 7 versions, including a funny version (see [Versions](#versions) below)

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; rail mount (full-length rail on the A3). Unique: the SUSAT 4× scope with a tritium-lit pointer and backup irons on top, the L123 (HK AG36) 40mm underbarrel launcher, rifle grenades (gas regulator has an "off" setting for them), and the L3A1 socket bayonet, offset so you can still fire with it fitted; the bayonet plus scabbard forms a wire cutter (ties into wire fences). Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 30 standard; real 60-round casket as extended; could share the M16's STANAG tiers

## Versions

7 versions after merging, including a funny version. 4.85×49mm is new to the ammo pool as an ultra-rare prototype round. The L86 LSW (later used as a marksman rifle), the 1950s EM-2 bullpup, and the EIW97 electronic-firing testbed went on the guns-to-add-later list.
