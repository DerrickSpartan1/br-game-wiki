# PK / PKM

| Spec | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Category | LMG (general-purpose machine gun) |
| Cartridge | Varies by version: 7.62×54mmR (main), 7.62×51mm, or 5.56×45mm |
| History | USSR; built by Mikhail Kalashnikov's team at Izhevsk to a 1955 requirement, beating Nikitin and Sokolov's design partly because it kept working after getting wet; adopted as the PK in 1961 and improved as the PKM in 1969, replacing the SGM and the RP-46 (see [DP-27](dp-27.md)); the PKP Pecheneg followed in 2001; still made in Russia, and reports in June 2024 said the PKZ will eventually replace the PKM; copied or licensed in China (Type 80), Serbia (Zastava M84), Bulgaria, Romania, Poland (UKM-2000), and Ukraine; fought from Vietnam and Afghanistan through the Gulf War, the Yugoslav and Chechen wars, Iraq, Syria, and the Russo-Ukrainian War, where Ukrainian volunteers bought 1,460 PKMs from Bulgaria in 2023 |
| Magazine (rounds) | Belts in 100-, 200-, or 250-round boxes, built from 25-round sections |
| Fire mode / rate | Auto only, about 600–800 RPM; gas-operated, long-stroke piston, rotating bolt; fires from an open bolt, so it has the open-bolt delay |

| Effective range | Real-world 1,000 m; in-game TBD |

## Real-world notes

PK: 9 kg with its bipod, 1,203 mm long, 605 mm barrel, 825 m/s; the PKM barrel assembly weighs 2.4 kg; it ejects to the left, unlike most Western machine guns; three-position gas regulator; a PKM barrel needs swapping or cooling after about 400 rounds of rapid fire (flavor only); the Pecheneg's heavy fixed barrel sits in a forced-air cooling sleeve like the Lewis gun's and is rated up to 10,000 rounds; the 100-round box hangs under the receiver for squad use

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 5 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). Full-power 7.62×54mmR on a belt, so it shares ammo with the [SVD](../battle-rifles/svd.md), [SVT-40](../battle-rifles/svt-40.md), and [DP-27](dp-27.md). Auto only, with the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; side-rail scope mount on night-sight (N) versions and the Pecheneg-N. Unique: the 200- and 250-round belt boxes, the tripod, and night sights. Mag tiers (all real belt boxes): 100 standard; 200 as extended; 250 as heavier extended; quick tiers TBD
