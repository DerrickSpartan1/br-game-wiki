## Real-world notes

About 4.31 kg; 1,105 mm long; 24 in barrel; about 853 m/s; rear aperture sight adjustable from 100 to 1,200 yd; early rifles (before mid-1940) used a gas trap at the muzzle, later ones a simpler drilled gas port; the safety sits at the front of the trigger guard, worked by the trigger finger; field-strips without tools; buttstock compartments hold cleaning gear; a winter trigger developed in Korea lets the shooter fire with gloves on; rumors that enemies listened for the clip's ping are unverified; about $85 each in WWII

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). The en bloc versions keep the clip's quirks (see [Rules](../../rules.md#en-bloc-clips)): the empty clip pings loud enough for nearby players to hear, and reloading early ejects the partial clip, with its leftover rounds going back to your inventory. The T20 and the box-mag conversion (box mags) and the Type 4 (internal mag) don't have these quirks.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount, except the sniper version, which spawns with its scope. Unique: the M7 grenade launcher on the muzzle (22mm rifle grenades, fired with M3 grenade cartridges in place of a ball clip), the M1905, M1, and M5 bayonets, and the M2 flash hider. Mag tiers: none, since it loads fixed 8-round en bloc clips; the T20 takes the BAR's 20-round mags and the box-mag conversion takes 20-round boxes (other tiers TBD)

## Versions

9 versions after merging. Commercial "Tanker" Garands, built on welded surplus receivers, merge into the short version. Ceremonial and demilitarized rifles aren't versions. Floro International's kit and the Brazilian and Argentine rifles converted to take box mags merge into one box-mag version (see [Rules](../../rules.md#spawned-version-is-fixed)). Beretta's BM 59 and the M14 grew from the Garand but are their own entries in the [queue](../../planning/queue.md); the T25 and T31 prototypes went on the [guns to add later](../../planning/add-later.md) list.
