## Real-world notes

M24: 5.4 kg empty without a scope (7.3 kg with scope, sling, and a loaded mag), 1,092 mm long, 610 mm barrel, 790 m/s with M118LR ammo; it had to hold 0.35 MOA through a 10,000-round barrel life, and the first ones carried a Leupold 10× fixed scope. M24A3: 5.6 kg, 1,181 mm, 686 mm barrel. M40A1: 6.57 kg; M40A3 and A5: 7.5 kg; all with 635 mm barrels and 777 m/s. The first M40's wooden stock warped, so the A1 moved to a McMillan fiberglass stock.

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 9 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). PUBG's M24. The internal-mag versions load one round at a time, like a tube; the detachable-mag versions reload by swapping mags. The .338 Lapua M24A3 is crate-only, since its head shot kills through a tier 4 helmet ([Starting damage](../../systems/starting-damage.md)).

## Attachments

DMR & sniper family attachments; native scope base. Unique: the M2010's suppressor and the M40A3's bipod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): the detachable-mag versions' 5 or 10 as standard; the rest TBD. The internal-mag versions take ammo loops instead.
