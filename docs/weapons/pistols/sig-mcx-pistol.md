## Real-world notes

An MCX with an arm brace (SIG's SBX or PCB) in place of a stock; the SPEAR LT has a Picatinny tail that takes a stock or a brace

## In-game traits

Moved to the 3rd slot like the other long-gun pistols. One roster entry that rolls one of 2 versions.
