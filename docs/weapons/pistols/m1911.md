## Real-world notes

Designed by John Browning; 5.03 in barrel (Government), 4.25 in (Commander), 3.5 in (Officer); 1,100 g; 253 m/s; grip and thumb safeties; about 4.3 million made including licensed copies. Main versions: M1911, M1911A1, Commander, Officer, M45 MEU(SOC), M45A1 (rails), British .455 contract, Colt Service Ace (.22 trainer), Korean War 7.62×25 conversions

## Versions

12 versions after merging, sorted by cartridge and frame size. Every Government 1911 can take a light, so the M45A1 merges in as a skin, and double-stack 2011s merge in because the mag tiers cover capacity. Skins: the Singer-made M1911A1 (only 500 made, an ultra-rare find), the M45A1's tan Cerakote, the WWII Parkerized finish with brown plastic grips, NRA-stamped guns, arsenal rebuild marks, Viet Cong copies, and commemoratives.
