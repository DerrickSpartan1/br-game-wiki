## Real-world notes

Bren Mk I: 10.3 kg, 1,160 mm long, 635 mm barrel, 743.7 m/s; Mk III and Mk IV: 8.7 kg, 1,090 mm, 565 mm barrel; vz. 26: 9.65 kg, 1,161 mm, 672 mm barrel, 764 m/s; quick-change barrel, swapped with the carry handle, with a spare in the kit; four-position gas regulator; the top-mounted mag pushes the sights to the left, so it fires from the right shoulder only; cases eject downward; gunners loaded 27–28 rounds in a 30-round mag to avoid jams (flavor only); Australian soldiers fired it from the hip while advancing; known for accuracy and a steady 500 RPM

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 10 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). It's an accurate, easy-to-control LMG with a slow rate and quick mag changes. The offset sights are cosmetic. Every version has the open-bolt delay.

## Attachments

AR/LMG/battle rifle family attachments; adapter scope mount, offset to the left. Unique: the 100-round pan (.303 versions only) and the tripod. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 30 standard (20 on the vz. 26 and L4); 100-round pan as extended on .303 versions; quick tiers TBD
