## Real-world notes

Kar98k: 600 mm barrel, sights from 100 to 2,000 m. Gewehr 98: 4.09 kg, 1,250 mm long, 740 mm barrel, 878 m/s with the 1903 pointed round (639 m/s with the old M/88). Kar98a: 3.5 kg, 1,090 mm long, 590 mm barrel. The Kriegsmodell of late 1944 cut corners to speed up production. The HUB-23 suppressor fired subsonic Nahpatrone ammo at 220 m/s, and 1,450,113 Schiessbecher 30mm grenade cups were made for the muzzle. About 132,000 sniper rifles got the 4× ZF39 scope, and more than 100,000 the 1.5× ZF41.

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 10 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). PUBG's Kar98k: hard-hitting and slow to cycle. It reloads like PUBG's: a full stripper clip from empty, or one round at a time to top off ([Ammo](../../systems/ammo.md#how-ammo-and-reloading-work)). Sniper versions keep their scope ([Built-in parts](../../systems/attachments.md#built-in-parts)).

## Attachments

DMR & sniper family attachments; adapter scope mount (the ZF39 and ZF41 versions come scoped). Unique: the HUB-23 suppressor, the Schiessbecher grenade cup, and the ZF39 and ZF41 scopes. Mag tiers: none, since it's fed by stripper clips; ammo loops speed up top-offs.
