## Real-world notes

SMLE Mk III and No. 4 Mk I: 640 mm barrel, 744 m/s, 4 kg. The Mk III was the first model for the pointed .303 round. The No. 4, adopted officially in 1941, is sturdier and easier to make. The No. 5 "Jungle Carbine" has a 475 mm barrel, a big flash hider, and a shorter stock, fires at 732 m/s, and weighs 3.2 kg. The Ishapore 2A1 is nearly identical to the SMLE outside but has stronger steel and a new extractor for the rimless 7.62 round, an 800 m rear sight, and a heavier mahogany stock. The Charlton is a gas-operated New Zealand conversion for the Home Guard, 7.3 kg unloaded.

## In-game traits

One roster entry that rolls one of 6 versions (see [Versions](#versions) below). A fast bolt and a 10-round box mag, so it reloads with mag swaps instead of clips. The No. 5's flash hider is built in ([Built-in parts](../../systems/attachments.md#built-in-parts)).

## Attachments

DMR & sniper family attachments; adapter scope mount (the No. 4 (T) comes scoped). Unique: the No. 5's flash hider and the Charlton's Bren mag. Mag tiers (best guess, review later): 10 standard; 12-round Ishapore mag as makeshift extended (2A1 only); 30-round Bren mag as extended (Charlton only); quick tiers TBD
